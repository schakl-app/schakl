{# Letterhead: the shape a Dutch agency invoice usually takes on paper. Behind all of it, the tenant's mark at a few percent opacity — a background, not a watermark: `CONCEPT` still stamps over the top when the document is a draft. Where it differs from `classic` is deliberate. Labels here are words in a two-column grid rather than a right-aligned stack, because this design gives the seller block half the header instead of a corner.

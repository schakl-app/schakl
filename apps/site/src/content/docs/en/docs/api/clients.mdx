The hub every other module attaches to: the client record, the people at that client, the paper trail of what changed, and the address lookup the forms use.

33 endpoints across 4 groups. A permission written `module.resource.action:own` is scoped: `:own` covers rows that are yours, `:any` covers everyone's, and `:any` satisfies a check for `:own`.

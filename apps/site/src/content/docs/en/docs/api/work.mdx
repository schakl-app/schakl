What the agency actually does, and what it bills: projects with budgets, task boards and checklists, the timer and the timesheet, and every contact moment on one timeline.

103 endpoints across 4 groups. A permission written `module.resource.action:own` is scoped: `:own` covers rows that are yours, `:any` covers everyone's, and `:any` satisfies a check for `:own`.

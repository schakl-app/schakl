**schakl.** is a self-hosted platform for agencies. Clients, contacts, projects, hours, leave,
tasks, domains, invoices, marketing figures and monthly reports live in one fast web app that also
installs as an app on your phone (PWA). You run it on your own server, under your own brand and
your own domain.

![My day: tiles you choose and order yourself, with today's work on the left and where things stand on the right.](/img/docs/en-dashboard.png)

## The client is the centre

Everything in schakl. attaches to the **client**: contacts, projects, domains, websites, hosting,
hours, touchpoints, agreements, invoices and reports. The client page shows a panel per topic —
disable a module and its panel disappears. One page keeps giving you the whole picture, however
large your installation grows.

## Modular from the core

Every capability is a **module** you enable or disable per organisation (*Instellingen → Modules*,
Settings → Modules). Each module brings its own screens, permissions and settings. What you do not
use, you never see: not in the menu, not on the client page and not in the permission list.

The core is open source (AGPL). Some modules run on a licence key; when it expires the module goes
read-only and you lose nothing. See [Licences](/en/docs/admin/licenses/).

## Your language, your brand

The whole app defaults to Dutch, with English built in; an extra language is one translation file.
Brand name, logo, favicon, colours and your own domain are set under *Instellingen → Huisstijl*
(Settings → Branding), and no rebuild is required. That same branding carries into your invoices,
your reports and every mail that goes out.

## How this documentation is laid out

- **Modules**: one page per thing you can switch on, from Clients to AI assistance. Each says where
  to find it, what you do with it, which permissions govern it, and what to know before it surprises
  you.
- **Integrations**: what schakl. does with outside services, and exactly what to fill in where.
- **Administration**: installing, upgrading, licences, e-mail, storage, single sign-on and
  two-factor verification.

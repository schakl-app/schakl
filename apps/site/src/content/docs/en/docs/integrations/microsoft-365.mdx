Your agency lives in Microsoft 365: the meetings are in Outlook, the deliverables in a SharePoint
document library or OneDrive, and the client history in your colleagues' mailboxes. The Microsoft
365 module pulls those three into the client record instead of asking anyone to work differently.
It is the Microsoft counterpart of [Google Workspace](/en/docs/integrations/google-workspace/):
the same three surfaces, the same rules, and both can be connected at once.

## Where to find it

- *Instellingen → Microsoft 365* (Settings → Microsoft 365, in the group **Integraties**): the
  agency-wide configuration, with the app registration, the surfaces, the OneDrive layout and the
  Outlook policy.
- *Instellingen → Mijn account* (Settings → My account), card **Microsoft-account**: where each
  employee connects themselves.
- After that you see it in **Agenda** (as the source **Outlook-agenda**), in the **OneDrive** panel
  on the client, project and task pages, and in **Interacties** (Interactions).

Microsoft 365 is a licensed module; under *Instellingen → Integraties* it is called **Microsoft
365**. See [Licenses](/en/docs/admin/licenses/).

## Your own app registration

Every agency registers its own app in Microsoft Entra. That keeps the consent inside your own
tenant and lets you decide which account types may connect.

<Steps>
1. In the Microsoft Entra admin center, create an **app registration** for your agency. Under
   supported account types, prefer **accounts in this organizational directory only**.

2. Under **API permissions**, add the delegated Microsoft Graph permissions `User.Read`,
   `offline_access`, `Calendars.ReadWrite`, `Files.ReadWrite.All` and `Mail.Read`. Grant admin
   consent if your tenant requires it.

3. Under **Certificates & secrets**, create a client secret and note its end date: an Entra secret
   expires, and on that day every connection stops refreshing until a new one is saved here.

4. Open *Instellingen → Microsoft 365*. At the top sit two read-only fields, each with a
   **Kopiëren** (Copy) button. Paste the **Redirect-URI** into your app registration under
   **Authentication → Web → Redirect URIs**. The **Meldings-URL** (notification URL) is where
   Microsoft posts calendar changes; if an access proxy sits in front of your host, let Microsoft's
   servers through to it.

5. Fill in the **Toepassings-id** (application/client ID), the **Clientgeheim** (client secret)
   and optionally the **Directory (tenant)** — `common`, `organizations`, or your own directory ID
   — and save. The secret is stored encrypted; leave the field empty on a later save to keep the
   stored one.

6. Under **Onderdelen** (Surfaces) tick what you use: **Agenda**, **OneDrive**, **Outlook-mail**.
   Only what is ticked here will be asked of your employees.
</Steps>

### OneDrive layout

Fill in the **Drive-ID** — the drive your client folders live in: a SharePoint document library's
drive ID, or a OneDrive's — the **Bovenliggende map voor klantmappen (ID)** (parent folder for
client folders) and optionally the **Sjabloonmap (ID)** (template folder). These are raw IDs;
there is no folder picker yet. Under **Automatiseringsaccount** (automation account) pick a
colleague who has already connected: the background work runs as that person. With **Maak
automatisch een map voor elke nieuwe klant** every new client gets its own folder, a copy of the
template. For clients that already existed there is **Mappen voor bestaande klanten** with the
button **Mappen aanmaken** (create folders), which also adopts an existing folder of the same name.

### Outlook policy

Under **Nieuwe gekoppelde e-mails** (newly matched e-mails) you choose between "Eigenaar keurt goed
voordat de inhoud wordt vastgelegd" (the owner approves before the content is stored, the default)
and "Direct vastleggen, inclusief inhoud" (store immediately, content included).
**Vervolgberichten in een gekoppeld gesprek** decides the same thing for a follow-up in a
conversation already filed against a client. **Ook e-mail tussen collega's oppakken** (also pick up
colleague-to-colleague mail) is off; internal mail always waits for review regardless. Reviewing
itself is described under [Interactions](/en/docs/modules/interactions/).

## Every employee connects themselves

Go to *Instellingen → Mijn account*, card **Microsoft-account**, and press **Microsoft koppelen**
(Connect Microsoft). Above the button, "Koppelen geeft toegang tot:" lists exactly what is being
asked for. If Outlook mail is on, there is a checkbox **Koppel ook mijn mailbox (Outlook)**.

Once connected, the card shows the connected address, the badge **Gekoppeld** (connected) or
**Aandacht nodig** (needs attention), a list of your other calendars to tick onto the Agenda, the
toggle **Mijn e-mail vastleggen in het CRM** (log my e-mail in the CRM), the field **Uitgesloten
Outlook-categorie** (messages carrying that Outlook category are never logged) and
**Microsoft-account ontkoppelen** (disconnect). Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens; Microsoft
offers no way to revoke them from here, so remove the app's access at myapps.microsoft.com as well.
Anything already logged stays.

<Aside type="caution" title="Signing in with Microsoft is not the same as connecting">
Signing in through Microsoft (single sign-on) and **Microsoft koppelen** are two separate grants.
Even in an agency that requires Microsoft sign-in, every employee still has to press this button
separately.
</Aside>

## What the three surfaces do

**Calendar.** The **Agenda** screen gains the source **Outlook-agenda** with your own events, read
from schakl.'s own copy and never fetched live from Microsoft. Extra calendars you ticked on the
account card each get their own colour. In the other direction, approved leave is written into
your own Outlook calendar as "Verlof: (leave type)" with the per-day hours in the notes, a planned
task as "(client): (title)", and a freelance availability day as free or busy time. Withdraw the
request, reject it or remove the block and the event disappears again.

**OneDrive.** The client, project and task pages carry a **OneDrive** panel. You browse the
client's folder with your own Microsoft 365 permissions, create subfolders with **Nieuwe map**,
upload files straight from the browser, and attach existing files with **Koppelen aan dit record**
(link to this record). **Ontkoppelen** removes only the link; **Verwijderen uit OneDrive** moves
the file to the recycle bin, where it can still be restored.

**Outlook mail.** Every five minutes the opted-in mailboxes are read forward from where the last
poll left off — the whole mailbox, so a message an inbox rule moved to a subfolder is seen too.
Messages where a participant is a known contact outside the agency land as "to review" under
**Interacties**; only after the mailbox owner approves is the content fetched and shared with the
team. A message the poller skipped can be fetched by hand with an Outlook on the web link, a
message ID or the Message-ID from "View message source", or found by searching your own mailbox
on address, subject and date. schakl. never sends mail through Outlook; outgoing mail uses the
transport under *Instellingen → E-mail*.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it opens | Default roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `microsoft.settings.manage` | Manage the Microsoft 365 integration, including **Mappen aanmaken** | Administrator |
| `microsoft.connection.manage` | Connect your own Microsoft account | Administrator, Member |
| `microsoft.calendar.read` | View Outlook calendar events | Administrator, Member |
| `microsoft.onedrive.read` | Browse OneDrive files | Administrator, Member |
| `microsoft.onedrive.write` | Link and upload OneDrive files | Administrator, Member |
| `microsoft.onedrive.manage` | Change or detach a record's OneDrive folder | Administrator |

Set these per role under *Instellingen → Rollen* (Settings → Roles).

## Good to know

- **The redirect URI follows your own hostname.** Move the workspace to a different domain and the
  new URI has to be registered on the app registration again, or Microsoft refuses the connect.
- **The client secret expires.** Entra caps a secret at 24 months. When it lapses, every
  connection flips to **Aandacht nodig**; save a new secret and reconnect.
- **The first Outlook poll fetches nothing.** It only records the starting point: existing mail
  is not imported retroactively.
- **The calendar only writes one way,** and reads a year ahead. Leave and planned tasks go out to
  Outlook; Outlook events come back read-only, and an event more than a year out appears when the
  cache next restarts.
- **OneDrive links are references.** schakl. never holds the bytes. Delete a file in OneDrive and
  the link points at nothing.
- **Automatic client folders quietly do nothing** unless all of it lines up: OneDrive on, the
  checkbox ticked, an automation account chosen, and a drive ID filled in.
- **Connected both Google and Microsoft?** A colleague with both accounts gets planned blocks in
  both calendars, the Agenda draws two sources, and the client hub draws a Drive panel beside a
  OneDrive panel. Mail is still one entry per e-mail, whichever mailbox read it.
- **Do not rotate the server key casually.** If the administrator rotates
  `SCHAKL_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, every stored connection lapses and everybody has to connect again.

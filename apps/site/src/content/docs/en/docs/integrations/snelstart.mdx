SnelStart is the bookkeeping and schakl. is the CRM, and it stays that way. Your clients become
relations, your issued invoices become sales bookings with the rendered PDF attached, and your
products become articles. One thing comes back, and it is exactly the thing SnelStart is
authoritative about: who has paid. That payment lands here as an ordinary payment line on the
invoice, so status, reminders and the client portal behave afterwards as they always have.

<Aside type="caution" title="Why this is beta (and why it is not)">
This integration was built and driven against a real SnelStart administration with a real
connection key: connecting, fetching ledger accounts, adopting relations, booking invoices with
the PDF attached, reading a payment back, and re-syncing until everything reported "unchanged".

Beta refers to two other things. The one-click connect has not been certified by SnelStart yet,
and the integration is young: it has not run in dozens of administrations, and administrations
differ (their own chart of accounts, their own day books, their own article settings). So start
with a handful of invoices and check in SnelStart that they look the way you expect.
</Aside>

## Where to find it

*Instellingen → SnelStart* (Settings → SnelStart), in the **Integrations** group. Each client's
state also shows on their own page, in the **SnelStart** panel: whether that client is paired with
a relation, what has been booked of their invoices, and what needs a look.

## Connecting

<Steps>

1. **Make sure you hold the right.** *Settings → Roles* → `snelstart.settings.manage`. By default
   only the **admin** role has this, and the client role never does.

2. **Get a connection key from SnelStart.** SnelStart Web → *Instellingen → Koppelingen* → new
   key. A connection key names exactly one administration; if you run several, you connect them
   separately.

3. **Paste the key.** *Settings → SnelStart* → **Connect administration**. schakl. immediately
   exchanges the key for a token and asks SnelStart for the administration details, so you see
   your own administration's name come straight back. If you do not, the key is wrong and nothing
   was stored.

4. **Fetch the reference data.** Ledger accounts, day books, VAT rates and countries are read once
   and kept. This is a read: nothing changes in your books.

5. **Choose the default ledger account.** An invoice line books to the account its VAT rate points
   at; for everything you have not mapped, schakl. uses this one. If you want it finer-grained,
   map an account per VAT rate.

6. **Review the relations.** schakl. lays your clients beside the administration's relations and
   shows which are already there and which are not. You confirm each pairing yourself; nothing is
   matched up automatically on the strength of a similar name.

7. **Book one invoice.** Take an issued invoice, push it, and check in SnelStart that the booking
   looks right: the amount, the VAT kind and the ledger account. If that is right, the rest is
   right.

</Steps>

## What goes out

Only **issued** invoices and credit notes. A draft never travels: it has no number yet and is
nobody's agreement.

An invoice becomes a **sales booking** (`verkoopboeking`), not a sales order. That is deliberate: a
sales order has SnelStart produce and send an invoice of its own, and then a second,
differently-designed invoice lands with your client. What schakl. renders and sends is the
document; SnelStart keeps the books, with the PDF attached.

The **VAT kind is looked up**, not assumed. The Dutch low rate was 6% until 2019 and 9% after, so a
percentage on its own says nothing: schakl. reads your own administration's rate table at the
invoice's date. A rate it cannot find there is reported rather than quietly resolved to something
plausible.

Filling in a relation **erases nothing**. What you entered inside SnelStart (the memo, the credit
limit, the direct-debit mandate) stays, and an empty field in schakl. is not an instruction to
clear it there.

## Never the same invoice twice

A duplicate invoice at a client is not a slip, it is an incident, so there are four locks on it:

1. schakl. remembers what it sent.
2. If it does not, it asks by invoice number.
3. If SnelStart refuses the number because it already exists, that is not a failure but an answer:
   the booking is looked up and adopted.
4. And if there is no answer at all (a timeout), it checks whether the booking is there before
   sending anything again.

A booking that was already there is **adopted, not overwritten**: somebody made it. If its amount
disagrees with your invoice, that lands on the screen as "needs a look" instead of being quietly
corrected.

## Who has paid

The bank statement is matched inside SnelStart, so SnelStart is the only place that knows an
invoice was paid. schakl. reads that back every night (or whenever you press the button yourself)
and turns it into an ordinary payment line on the invoice: the status flips to paid, reminders
stop, and the client portal is right again. A partial payment counts, because somebody who paid
half is not yet somebody who paid.

Only money *in* is booked. Reversing a payment is a person's decision and does not happen here on
its own.

## Permissions

| Right | What it opens | Default for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `snelstart.settings.manage` | Connect the administration, replace the key, choose the ledger accounts and remove the connection | Admin |
| `snelstart.sync.run` | Read the administration: fetch reference data, review relations and pull payments back. Nothing changes inside SnelStart | Admin |
| `snelstart.ledger.write` | Write into the books. Pushing invoices additionally requires the right to edit invoices | Admin |

Reading and writing are deliberately separate: reading and reviewing is a different act from
changing something in your agency's books.

## When something goes wrong

Every sync records what happened, row by row: what worked, what stayed unchanged, what failed and
what needs a look. A failed row can be retried without redoing the rest.

If it fails overnight, whoever administers the connection hears about it straight away. If you
would rather they did not, switch that notification off in your notification preferences; the
difference from "we never offered it" is exactly the point.

<Aside type="tip" title="Two keys, two owners">
There are two secrets in play and they belong to different people. The **connection key**
(koppelsleutel) is yours and names your administration; you replace it yourself in SnelStart Web.
The **subscription key** belongs to the installation and to whoever administers it. SnelStart
refuses both with the same kind of error, so schakl. keeps them apart and says which of the two it
was. If you are told it is the subscription key, re-issuing your connection key will not help.
</Aside>

## Good to know

- **Quotes do not travel.** A quote has no accounting consequence yet, and SnelStart would turn it
  into a second, differently-designed quote. Sales invoices and credit notes do travel.
- **One connection is one administration.**
- **One-click connecting is waiting on certification.** Today you paste a connection key. If you
  run your own installation, that button does not appear at all: SnelStart has never heard of your
  hostname and could never post anything to it.
- **Hours, projects and subscriptions do not travel as such.** What goes across is the invoice that
  comes out of them.
- **Syncing a second time writes nothing.** "Unchanged" is the normal answer.
- **Client numbers need not collide.** If your client number is already a relation code in
  SnelStart, schakl. looks at whose code it is: if it is the same client, that relation is adopted,
  and otherwise SnelStart allocates a number of its own. The relation is the point; the shared
  number is a convenience.
- **Peppol and e-invoicing networks are separate from this.**

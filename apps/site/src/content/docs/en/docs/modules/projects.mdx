A project is a client engagement with a budget attached. It is the thing hours get logged
against, the thing tasks hang off, and the place you find out whether you are still inside the
agreement — before the month is over.

![Projects with their budget: the bar takes its colour from what has been spent.](/img/docs/en-projects.png)

## Where to find it

**Projecten** (Projects) in the main menu. Every client also carries a **Projecten** panel on
their client record, with a **＋ Nieuw project** button that fills the client in for you.
Projects is a licensed module: you switch it on under *Instellingen → Modules* (Settings →
Modules), and *Instellingen → Licentie* (Settings → License) decides whether it is covered.

## Creating a project

The fields that decide how a project behaves:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Klant** (Client) | What the project hangs off; may be empty for internal work |
| **Toegewezen medewerkers** (Assigned employees) | One **Verantwoordelijke** (responsible) plus as many others as you like |
| **Status** | Actief, On hold, Afgerond (completed) or Gearchiveerd (archived) |
| **Budget (uren)** | The hours budget logged time is measured against |
| **Budgetperiode** (Budget period) | Whole project, per month, per week or per day |
| **Budget (bedrag)** | The money budget, in your organisation's currency |
| **Standaard facturabel** (Billable by default) | Decides how new time entries on this project start |
| **Startdatum** / **Einddatum** | Free to fill in |

On creation the project inherits the client's **Verantwoordelijke** (responsible) as its own
responsible, not the whole roster of staff attached to that client. That person is also the
default assignee of new tasks on the project.

The **Omschrijving** (description) is a rich-text field: you can @mention colleagues in it and
reference other tasks with `#`.

## The budget bar

The **Budgetperiode** decides what "logged" means. *Hele project* counts everything from the
start; *Per maand* resets on the first of the month, *Per week* on Monday, *Per dag* every day.
The boundary is **midnight in your organisation's timezone** (*Instellingen → Huisstijl*,
Settings → Branding), not UTC — so an hour logged at 23:30 on 31 January counts in January.

![The project page carries the budget beside the hours, tasks and invoices hanging off it.](/img/docs/en-project-detail.png)

The project page shows *Geboekt deze maand* ("logged this month", or this week, today, or just
*Geboekt*) with the bar underneath. The bar stops at 100 %; the number does not, so a project
at 140 % reads as 140 %. The same figures appear on the list in the **Beschikbare uren**
(available hours) column.

With the right permissions you also get **Facturabele waarde** (billable value) and **Kosten
(medewerkerstarieven)** (cost at employee rates). Every hour is priced at the rate of whoever
logged it; hours booked by people with no rate are called out separately and left out of the
total. There is no project-level rate.

<Aside type="tip">
Assigned staff are notified when an active, budgeted project passes **75 %** and again at
**100 %** — once per budget period, so a monthly budget warns again next month. Each person
turns that on or off under *Instellingen → Meldingen* (Settings → Notifications).
</Aside>

## Hours that come from a subscription

Link a project to a live subscription that carries **Inbegrepen uren** (included hours) — in
the subscriptions module, field *Gekoppelde projecten* (linked projects) — and the hours budget
comes from that agreement. Several subscriptions add up. **Budget (uren)** then greys out, the
budget period is fixed to *Per maand*, and the page says **Uren uit abonnement** with the
agreement's name beside it.

<Aside type="caution">
The moment a project is linked to a subscription, **Standaard facturabel** is switched off:
work a retainer already pays for should not be invoiced twice. It stays a default, not a lock —
you can switch it back on for extra work agreed outside the retainer, and the flag on any one
time entry is always the logger's call. The change is written to the project's activity trail.
</Aside>

## On the project page

- **Taken** (to-dos): the project's own list. Add a line, tick it off, and drag it by the ⋮⋮
  handle to reorder. Done-of-total sits top right.
- **Documenten**: upload, download and delete files; every upload lands on the activity trail.
- **Contactmoment vastleggen** (log interaction) in the header, when the interactions module is
  on: the project and its client are already filled in.
- Panels contributed by other modules: **Uren** (hours), **Contactmomenten** and
  **Abonnementen**.
- At the bottom the **activity trail**: who changed what and when, with the old and new value.

## The list

Search, a client filter and a **Mijn projecten** (my projects) toggle that keeps only the
projects you are on. You choose your own columns; that choice is personal and is remembered.
Sorting works on name, status, assigned employee, budget and the dates.

The ✎ switches the checkboxes on and lets you change a whole selection at once: **status**,
**client** and **billable by default**. Budgets are deliberately absent — a budget is agreed
per project. Deleting works from the same selection.

An import recognises an existing project by its **name** and updates it; the rest are created.
The `company` column matches on the exact client name. See
[Import & export](/en/docs/modules/import-export/).

## Permissions

| Permission | Held by default |
| --- | --- |
| `projects.project.read` | Administrator, Member, Client |
| `projects.project.write` | Administrator |
| `projects.project.delete` | Administrator |

The owner role holds everything. You set this per role under *Instellingen → Rollen* (Settings
→ Roles). The money figures on the project page hang off two further permissions:
*Urenoverzicht en rapportages bekijken* (`time.report.read`) and *Uurtarieven bekijken* for
everyone (`leave.rate.read:any`). Without both you see the hours bar but no amounts.

## Good to know

- **Deleting a project takes nothing with it.** Tasks and time entries stay; they simply lose
  their project link.
- **The budget bar counts the whole team's hours**, not only yours.
- **Custom fields** can be added per project under *Instellingen → Aangepaste velden*
  (Settings → Custom fields); they appear in the form, on the page and in the export.
- The **Dashboard** carries a **Projectbudgetten** (project budgets) widget: the budgeted
  projects burning hottest.

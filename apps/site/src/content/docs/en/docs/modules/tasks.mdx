Tasks are the work itself: what has to happen, for which client, by whom and when. The board
groups on the statuses your agency configures, not on a list we decided for you.

![The task board, grouped by the statuses you set up yourself.](/img/docs/en-tasks.png)

## Where to find it

**Taken** (Tasks) in the main menu. Every client record also carries a **Taken** panel, and
every project page a to-do list. The settings screens are:

- *Instellingen → Taakstatussen* (Settings → Task statuses): the columns of your board
- *Instellingen → Taaklabels* (Settings → Task labels): coloured labels
- *Taken → Sjablonen* (Tasks → Templates): task templates and checklist templates on one screen

Tasks is part of schakl.'s open core, so it needs no licence key.

## The board

Opening **Taken** shows **your** tasks first, not everyone's. To see the rest, pick another
assignee or **Geen** (None) in the person picker.

You can filter on client, project, assignee, label and deadline (**Over tijd** overdue,
**Vandaag** today, **Deze week** this week), plus free text. Rows are grouped per status;
finished statuses start folded away. You choose your own columns and that choice is remembered;
sorting orders rows inside each group.

The ✎ switches the checkboxes on and lets you change a whole selection at once: **status**,
**assignee**, **priority**, **project**, **client** and **deadline**. Rows that are refused
(a task whose deadline needs a reason, say) are reported per row; the rest still go through.
**Nieuwe taak** (New task) creates the task straight away and drops you on its page in edit
mode; there is no separate creation form.

## Setting up statuses

Under *Instellingen → Taakstatussen* you rename, recolour and reorder the columns of your
board, or add your own. Each status carries three flags:

- **Afgerond** (finished): an end state. It decides when a task counts as done, folds away on
  the board, and triggers a recurrence set to "after completion".
- **Standaard** (default): where new tasks start.
- **Contactmoment vereist**: this status is only reachable through a logged contact moment.

<Aside type="caution">
A status can only be deleted once no tasks are left in it, and the last remaining status can
never be deleted. Move the tasks out first.
</Aside>

## A task

On the task page, **Bewerken** (Edit) opens the definition: **Status**, **Prioriteit**
(Laag/Normaal/Hoog), **Toegewezen aan** (assignee), **Project**, **Klant**, **Vervaldatum**
(due date) and **Tijdbudget** (time budget) in minutes. The time budget is shown against the
hours logged on the task, as "x left" or "x over budget".

![A task with a checklist: ticking an item is using the page rather than editing it, so there is no save button.](/img/docs/en-task-detail.png)

- **Labels**: coloured, org-wide, and creatable from inside a task.
- **Checklists**: several per task, each with a markdown description, and items that may carry
  their own description. **Uit sjabloon** (From template) pulls in an existing checklist;
  **Opslaan als sjabloon** (Save as template) puts this one in the shared library.
- **Links & bijlagen**: URLs plus real file uploads.
- **Reacties** (comments): with @mentions of colleagues and contacts and `#` references to other
  tasks. Editing and deleting are allowed; an edited comment is marked "bewerkt".
- **Activiteit**: everything that happened to the task, from creation to an extended deadline
  and its reason. The name of whoever did it is snapshotted, so it stays readable after the
  account is gone.

Tick the last open item and schakl. offers to move the task to its finished status.

<Aside type="caution">
Moving a deadline **later** demands a reason. That reason is written into the task's activity
trail; without one the save is refused.
</Aside>

### Two switches that reach beyond the task

- **Alleen afsluiten met een contactmoment** (close only with a contact moment): this one task
  may only reach a finished status through a contact moment designated as its closer.
  Independent of the status flag, so a single task can demand it without the whole status doing
  so.
- **Zichtbaar voor klant** (visible to client): off by default. When on, a portal contact of
  that client can see the task and comment on it.

A task can also be assigned to a **contact of its own client** instead of to an employee:
"waiting on the client". Never to both at once.

## Planning it on the calendar

A deadline is not the same thing as the moment you work on it. Under **Agenda → Nieuw →
Inplannen** (Calendar → New → Schedule), and from the **Planning** panel on the task itself,
you plan blocks:

<Steps>
1. Pick the task; its time budget and deadline are shown beside it.
2. Choose **Voor wie** (for whom). Yourself always; a colleague only with the matching
   permission.
3. Fill in **Datum**, **Starttijd**, **Duur (minuten)** and optionally a **Notitie**.
4. The block appears on the Agenda and can be dragged to another day.
5. Once the block has passed it offers **Uren registreren** (log time). That creates the real
   time entry and marks the block as logged, so you never write it twice.
</Steps>

One task may carry several blocks; with the Google integration connected they also appear in
Google Calendar.

## Recurrence

A task can repeat **Dagelijks**, **Wekelijks**, **Maandelijks**, **Per kwartaal** or
**Jaarlijks**, in one of two modes:

- **Na afronden** (after completion): the next task appears the moment you finish this one.
- **Op schema** (on schedule): it is created automatically on the interval, even if this one is
  still open.

Labels and checklists carry over to the next occurrence.

## Templates

**Taken → Sjablonen** holds two libraries. A **takensjabloon** (task template) is a named set of
tasks, each with a priority, **Deadline na** so many days, a time budget, an assignee or
**Verantwoordelijke van de klant** (the client's responsible person, resolved at apply time),
and a checklist. Apply it **Handmatig** (manually) from a client record with **Sjabloon
toepassen**, or automatically **Bij klantstatus** (on client status): the tasks appear as soon
as a client reaches that status. A subscription type can roll its templates out the first time
an agreement becomes active. **Checklistsjablonen** (checklist templates) are reusable
checklists anyone can attach to a task.

## Reminders

A daily job announces what is overdue and what falls inside your own threshold; you set that
per person under *Instellingen → Meldingen* (Settings → Notifications), field **Bijna verlopen
(dagen)** (due soon, in days). A task announces itself once per deadline, and again as soon as
someone moves it.

## Permissions

| Permission | Held by default |
| --- | --- |
| `tasks.task.read` | Administrator, Member, Client |
| `tasks.task.create` | Administrator, Member |
| `tasks.task.write` | Administrator (any), Member (own) |
| `tasks.task.delete` | Administrator |
| `tasks.comment.write` | Administrator (any), Member and Client (own) |
| `tasks.schedule.read` / `tasks.schedule.write` | Administrator (anyone's), Member (their own) |
| `tasks.label.write` · `tasks.status.write` | Administrator |
| `tasks.template.write` · `tasks.template.apply` · `tasks.checklist_template.write` | Administrator |

For a task, "own" means **the task is assigned to you**. A member therefore edits their own
tasks and not somebody else's. All of it is adjustable per role under *Instellingen → Rollen*
(Settings → Roles).

## Good to know

- **Deleting a task takes its comments, checklists, links and planned blocks with it.** Hours
  already logged survive; they simply lose their task link.
- **Import always creates new tasks.** Task titles legitimately repeat, so there is no honest
  key to update on. The `assignee` column matches on the employee's e-mail address, and `status`
  is checked against your own status list. Exporting works normally; see
  [Import & export](/en/docs/modules/import-export/).
- **The Sjablonen tab only appears for people allowed to manage templates**; a portal client
  never reaches it.
- The **Dashboard** carries the widgets **Mijn openstaande taken** (my open tasks) and
  **Openstaande taken per project / klant**, and the **Agenda** the feeds **Ingeplande taken**
  (scheduled tasks) and **Taakdeadlines** (red once something is overdue).

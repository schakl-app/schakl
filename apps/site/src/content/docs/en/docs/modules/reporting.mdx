Every month each client has to hear how things are going, and pulling that together by hand out
of Analytics, Search Console and a rank tracker eats a day. The reporting module produces the
whole document — numbers, charts, prose in your own voice, your branding — per client, on a
schedule, with the PDF attached to the mail.

![Reports with their state: drafted, reviewed, published, sent.](/img/docs/en-reports.png)

## Where to find it

- **Rapportages** (Reports) in the main nav: every document, newest period first, with filters
  on client and audience.
- The **Rapportage** (Reporting) panel on the client page: the latest reports, the schedule, the
  recipients, and the buttons **Nieuwe rapportage** (New report), **Instellen** (Set up) and
  **Alle rapportages** (All reports).
- *Instellingen → Rapportage* (Settings → Reporting): the tone of voice, the templates and the
  organisation-wide schedule. One client's own profile deliberately does not live here but on
  the client itself.

Reporting is a licensed module; switch it on under *Instellingen → Modules* (Settings →
Modules). See [Licenses](/en/docs/admin/licenses/).

## What has to be in place first

<Steps>
1. Under *Instellingen → AI* (Settings → AI), fill in a provider and API key and switch on the
   feature **Briefing & rapporten** (Briefings & reports). All prose runs through your own AI
   account, budget and usage meter.

2. Connect the data per client through the [marketing module](/en/docs/modules/marketing/).
   Without linked sources there is nothing to report, and the screen says so.

3. Walk *Instellingen → Rapportage*: **Planning** (Schedule), **Tone of voice** and **Sjablonen**
   (Templates). On first use a Dutch default voice is already there, including a list of words
   it must never use.

4. Open a client, press **Instellen** on the **Rapportage** panel and fill in the profile.
</Steps>

## Two documents about the same numbers

A period can carry two documents: the **Maandrapportage** (Monthly report) the client reads, and
the **Interne analyse** (Internal analysis), your own working document with risks, gaps and
whatever may fall outside the agreed scope. They read the same numbers and differ only in prose
and in which sections run: the site audit, **Wat we deze maand oppakken** (What we are picking
up this month) and **Open vragen** (Open questions) appear in the internal analysis alone. That
one is never sent to a client and never published to the portal.

## Schedule, voice and template

*Instellingen → Rapportage → Planning* holds the defaults every client inherits: **Hoe vaak**
(How often: not automatically, monthly, quarterly), **Dag van de maand** (Day of the month,
default the 5th, capped at the 28th), **Uur** (Hour), **Vergelijken met** (Compare with), **Na
het genereren** (After generating: review first, or send straight away), **Publiceren in het
klantportaal** (Publish to the client portal) and a **Afsluitende regel** (Closing line). The 5th
is the default on purpose: attribution in Analytics keeps moving for days after a month ends,
and Search Console finalises later still.

A **tone of voice** is a row you edit, not code: **Schrijfinstructies** (Writing instructions) in
your own words, **Deze woorden nooit gebruiken** (Never use these words, one per line) and
**Deze formuleringen hebben de voorkeur** (Prefer these formulations). Several can exist side by
side, because not every client reads the same register. A **template** decides the look:
**Voor wie** (Audience), **Accentkleur** (Accent colour, blank means your brand colour), an
**Openingsalinea** (Opening paragraph) and the **Onderdelen** (Sections), the chapters the
enabled modules contribute, reorderable and switchable here.

## The client profile

Under **Over deze klant** (About this client) you fill in what is true about them: **Wat ze
doen** (What they do), **Hun doelen** (Their goals), **SEO-focus**, **SEA-focus**,
**Belangrijkste diensten** (Main services), **Prioritaire pagina's** (Priority pages),
**Conversiedoelen** (Conversion goals), **Scope** and **Niet noemen** (Do not mention). These are
facts, not instructions: they travel to the model as data, and the tone of voice decides the
phrasing.

Below that sit **Ontvangers** (Recipients, ticked from the contacts, plus **Andere adressen**,
other addresses one per line), **Toon, taal en ontwerp** (Voice, language and design), this
client's **Planning**, **Ook de interne analyse maken** (Also produce the internal analysis) and
**Rapportage actief voor deze klant** (Reporting active for this client). Anything left on
**Standaard volgen** (Follow the default) inherits the organisation setting; the screen shows the
resolved result and the next run date.

## Generate, review, send

A report is always queued, never produced inline. Start one with **Nieuwe rapportage** on the
client, or with **Deze periode genereren** (Generate this period) on *Rapportages* for every
configured client at once; each client runs as its own job, so one slow source does not take the
rest with it. Status reads **Concept** (Draft), **Wordt gemaakt** (Generating), **Klaar om na te
kijken** (Ready to review), **Verstuurd** (Sent) or **Mislukt** (Failed).

On the review screen you read the summary and every section paragraph. You can edit the text by
hand (that section is then marked **met de hand aangepast**, edited by hand, and survives a
regenerate) or press **Opnieuw schrijven** (Rewrite) to have one section written again. **Het
document** (The document) previews exactly what the PDF prints, **Pdf** downloads it, and at the
top sits **Goed om te weten voordat je verstuurt** (Worth knowing before you send): this run's
warnings, which the client never sees.

**Naar de klant sturen** (Send to the client) mails the PDF to the profile's recipients; **In het
portaal publiceren** (Publish to the portal) makes it visible in the client portal, independent
of mailing and reversible. The covering mail is reworded under *Instellingen → E-mail* (Settings
→ E-mail), template **Maandrapportage**, with the variables `{brand}`, `{client}`, `{contact}`,
`{period}`, `{sender_name}` and `{link}`. The sender name resolves from the client's responsible
employee.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it opens | Default roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reporting.report.read` | Read reports (`:any` is staff, drafts included; `:own` is a client's own published reports) | Administrator, Member (`:any`), Client (`:own`) |
| `reporting.report.write` | Generate, edit prose, rewrite a section, delete | Administrator, Member |
| `reporting.report.send` | Publish and e-mail to clients | Administrator |
| `reporting.internal.read` | Read the internal analysis | Administrator, Member |
| `reporting.profile.manage` | Manage a client's reporting profile | Administrator, Member |
| `reporting.settings.manage` | Manage tone of voice, templates and schedule | Administrator |

Sending is deliberately not part of writing: a junior can write the month while the send button
stays with whoever owns the relationship.

## Good to know

- **A period is always a whole calendar month or quarter**, resolved in the organisation's own
  timezone. A run on 5 August covers 1–31 July and is filed as July.
- **Re-running does not produce a second document**: a report is unique per client, audience and
  period, so a second run updates the same one. Every number it prints is frozen into it, so a
  March report still says next December what it said then.
- **Banned words are checked after generation, not merely requested.** Whatever slips through
  lands on the warnings, where you see it before the client does.
- **A sent report cannot be deleted and its prose cannot be edited.** If sending fails, the
  report keeps its ready status and can be retried.
- **Recipients are stored flat on the profile**, not joined live from the contacts. When someone
  leaves the client, they stay on the list until somebody takes them off.
- **With no AI configured, or over the monthly budget, the report is still produced**: the
  numbers, no prose, and a warning that says so.
- **An expired licence makes the module read-only** and stands the schedule down; every report
  already produced still opens and downloads.

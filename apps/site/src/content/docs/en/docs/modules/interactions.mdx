Every touchpoint with a client lands on one timeline — the meetings, calls and notes you log
yourself, emails you upload, and (with the Google module) the email out of your team's mailboxes.
A colleague picking up an account can read the history instead of asking around for it.

## Where to find it

- Main menu **Interacties** (Interactions): every contactmoment, across all clients.
- The **Contactmomenten** (Interactions) panel on the client, project, task and contact pages.
- The **E-mails te beoordelen** (Emails to review) widget on your dashboard, with its
  *Beoordeel alle …* (Review all …) link.
- *Instellingen → Contactmoment-typen* (Settings → Interaction kinds): the kinds your team can
  log.

<Aside type="caution">
Interactions is a licensed module. Switch it on under *Instellingen → Modules* (it is listed
there as **Contactmomenten**, and appears in the menu as **Interacties**) and make sure your
licence covers it. Without cover the module goes read-only after the grace period: the timeline
stays readable, but nothing new lands on it.
</Aside>

## Logging an interaction

The **Contactmoment vastleggen** (Log interaction) button sits top right on Interacties, and the
same form lives inside every Contactmomenten panel. In order:

- **Type**: from your own list. Email cannot be chosen here — only a real message may call itself
  one.
- **Datum** (Date) and **Tijd** (Time).
- **Voeg aan mijn uren toe** (Add to my hours): a checkbox above the subject. Time then becomes
  **Van** (From), a **Tot** (To) appears beside it, and the duration runs along; on save a time
  entry lands on your own timesheet, linked to this moment and carrying the client, project and
  subject. New moments only, and never for a note.
- **Onderwerp** (Subject): optional. Left empty, the row is titled by its kind ("Telefoongesprek").
- **Contactpersonen** (Contact persons): everyone who was in it, not one person. Typing searches,
  an unknown name is created on the spot, and the first in the row is the lead. Filtering on
  someone finds the moments they were in, not only the ones they led.
- **Gekoppeld aan** (Linked to): **Klant** (Client), **Project** and **Taak** (Task). Pick a
  project or a task and the client is taken from it, which the field says out loud.
- **Taak hiermee afronden** (Close task with this): available on a linked task, with a choice of
  your completed statuses.
- **Richting** (Direction — Incoming, Outgoing or Not applicable) and **Notities** (Notes), rich
  text with `@` mentions of colleagues and contacts.

## The list

At the top sit the tabs **Alle** (All) and **Te beoordelen** (To review), a kind filter and the
*Vastgelegd door* (Logged by) filter. That last one starts on **Mijn contactmomenten** (Mine) and
**Iedereen** (Everyone) is one click away, but naming a specific colleague needs the *View
everyone's interactions* permission. For dates there is a week stepper (*Vorige week*, *Deze
week*, *Volgende week*), a month picker and an explicit **Van** – **Tot en met** range with
*Datums wissen* (Clear dates) beside it. Search covers subject, snippet and body. The columns are
Onderwerp, Type, Gekoppeld aan, Vastgelegd door and Wanneer (Subject, Type, Linked to, Logged by,
When).

Per row, ⋯ offers *Bewerken* (Edit), *Verplaatsen / koppelen* (Move / link, onto a different
client, project or task, with inline create) and *Verwijderen* (Delete). On an email, *Goedkeuren*
(Approve), *Toewijzen…* (Assign…), *Afwijzen* (Reject) and *Aan gesprek toevoegen* (Add to
conversation) join them.

## Email from Gmail

With the Google module connected, schakl. polls the enrolled mailboxes every five minutes.
Messages where a participant is a known contact land as **Te beoordelen**. The team then sees only
the metadata: participants, subject and a snippet. Only once the mailbox owner approves is the
body fetched and shared.

<Steps>

1. Open **Interacties** and click the **Te beoordelen** tab (or use the dashboard widget).
2. Click a row to read the message.
3. **Goedkeuren** shares the content, **Toewijzen…** first files it onto the right client or
   project, **Afwijzen** removes it.
4. Several at once: switch the checkboxes on with the ✎, tick rows (shift-click selects a run) and
   choose Approve, Assign or Reject. You get a per-row outcome rather than all-or-nothing.

</Steps>

The policy lives under *Instellingen → Google Workspace → Gmail-beleid* (Gmail policy). Under
**Nieuwe gekoppelde e-mails** (New matched emails) you choose between "Eigenaar keurt goed voordat
de inhoud wordt vastgelegd" (the default) and "Direct vastleggen, inclusief inhoud"; *Ook e-mail
tussen collega's oppikken* is off by default, and internal email always waits for review.

<Aside type="caution">
Rejecting deletes the email from schakl. *and* stops it being logged again. With "Negeer ook
toekomstige e-mails in dit gesprek" that applies to the whole conversation. There is no undo.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">
An email that has not been reviewed is private to its mailbox owner. Not even an admin can decide
about it: that escape deliberately does not exist. If someone leaves with a full review queue,
nobody else can work through it.
</Aside>

The first poll of a newly connected mailbox imports nothing — it only records the starting point.
Existing mail is not pulled in retroactively.

## Uploading an email

Without a Gmail connection (or for a message from another mailbox) there is **E-mail uploaden**
(Upload email). Export the message from your mail program as `.eml`; subject, participants, date,
body and attachments are read out of the file and the formatting is kept. If the message is
already on the timeline, schakl. says so and offers *Toch vastleggen* (Log anyway). Attachments
that are too large or of a disallowed type are skipped, with a message telling you how many.

## Conversations

Emails from one conversation fold into a single row on the list, with a message count. The detail
window expands the whole conversation, and *Toon eerdere berichten* (Show earlier messages) reveals
the quoted history. If a stray message belongs to it after all, *Aan gesprek toevoegen* glues it
on by hand. A signature image belongs to the body rather than to the attachments; images loaded
from an external server are dropped, because a tracking pixel is an image too.

## Interaction kinds

*Instellingen → Contactmoment-typen* holds the vocabulary. It ships with E-mail, Online afspraak,
Afspraak op locatie, Telefoongesprek and Notitie (Email, Online meeting, On-site meeting, Call,
Note). You add your own with a Dutch and an English label and an ordering.

**E-mail** is a system kind: it can be relabelled but never deleted or deactivated. A kind already
in use cannot be deleted either; deactivate it instead, so it disappears from the pickers while
the history stays correct.

## Permissions

Set per role under *Instellingen → Rollen* (Settings → Roles). By default:

| Permission | What it does | Held by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `interactions.interaction.read` | View interactions | Administrator, Member |
| `interactions.interaction.read_all` | View everyone's interactions | Administrator |
| `interactions.interaction.write` | Log and edit | Administrator (any), Member (own) |
| `interactions.interaction.delete` | Delete | Administrator (any), Member (own) |
| `interactions.interaction.review` | Review logged emails (own mailbox) | Administrator, Member |
| `interactions.kind.read` | View interaction kinds | Administrator, Member |
| `interactions.kind.manage` | Manage interaction kinds | Administrator |

The Client role holds none of these, so a client-portal login never sees the timeline. And
`review` has no "anyone's" variant: even with it, you only ever decide about your own mailbox.

## Good to know

- Interactions cannot be imported or exported; this list carries no Export and Import buttons. Its
  selection mode offers reviewing only.
- A task can carry the requirement that it may only be closed with an interaction ("Contactmoment
  vereist"). Log the moment and tick *Taak hiermee afronden*.
- Deleting a project or a task leaves the interaction standing; only the link goes. So does the
  name of whoever logged it, even after that colleague leaves.
- Notifications: you can be alerted on *An email is waiting for my review* and *I am mentioned in
  an interaction*.

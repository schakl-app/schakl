An agency runs on things other people do: a task lands on a colleague, a project eats through its
budget, somebody requests leave, your timesheet is still empty on Friday. schakl. turns those into a
bell people actually read, and lets each person decide, per event, whether it also reaches their
inbox or their Slack, and whether it arrives now or as one bundle a day.

## Where to find it

- The **bell** in the top bar, on every screen, with the unread count.
- **Meldingen** (Notifications), the full list, via "Alles bekijken" (View all) in the bell.
- *Instellingen → Meldingen* (Settings → Notifications), under "Mijn instellingen" (My settings):
  your own preferences and your own channels. Every member has this screen.
- *Instellingen → Standaardmeldingen* (Settings → Notification defaults), in the group "Communicatie
  & koppelingen" (Communication & integrations): what everyone inherits, plus the organisation's
  shared channels. Admins only.

## The bell and the list

The bell shows your most recent unread items, with **Alles gelezen** (Mark all read),
**Meldingsinstellingen** (Notification settings) and **Alles bekijken**. The *Meldingen* screen has
the columns **Melding** (Notification), **Onderwerp** (Subject), **Door** (By) and **Wanneer**
(When), grouped per day (Vandaag, Gisteren). You filter on **Alles** / **Ongelezen** (All / Unread)
and by record type (Taak, Project, Klant, Verlof, Urenregistratie, Contactmoment). Each row has
**Markeer als gelezen** and back again; the top of the list carries **Alles als gelezen markeren**.

## What you get told about

There are twenty-one events, grouped the way the settings screen groups them:

| Group | For example |
| --- | --- |
| Taken (8) | Someone @mentions me in a comment |
| Projecten (3) | A project is eating its budget |
| Klanten (3) | A client changes status |
| Verlof (3) | Someone requests leave |
| Uren (2) | My timesheet is still empty |
| Contactmomenten (2) | An e-mail is waiting for my review |

An automation rule can also send a notification. That one is not in the table below: you switch a
rule off in the rule itself.

## The matrix: who gets what, and when

Under *Instellingen → Meldingen*, the section **Per gebeurtenis** (Per event) holds one row per
event, with:

- **In-app**: an **Aan** (On) tick, a **Bezorging** (Delivery) and a **Vertraging (min)** (Delay in
  minutes).
- **E-mail**: a **Bezorging** and a **Vertraging (min)**.
- One column per channel you connected yourself.
- **Waarde van** (Value from): where this row's value came from, as **Standaard** (Default),
  **Organisatie** (Organisation) or **Eigen instelling** (Your setting). **Terug naar overgenomen**
  (Reset to inherited) drops your own override again.

Delivery is always **Uit** (Off), **Direct** (Immediately), **Elk uur** (Hourly), **Dagelijks
overzicht** (Daily digest) or **Wekelijks overzicht** (Weekly digest). "Alles instellen…" (Set all…)
above a channel column sets that whole column at once; **Alle e-mail instellen op** does it for the
e-mail column.

<Aside type="caution">
Everything outside the app is a subset of in-app. Untick a row's **Aan** box and that event no longer
leaves the app, whatever its E-mail column or any channel column says. This is by far the most common
reason for "nothing arrived". For a shared channel the same rule applies to the **organisation**
in-app row: if nobody receives it in the app, nothing reaches the room either.
</Aside>

By default everything is on in-app. A short list arrives immediately (assignments, @mentions, overdue
tasks, tasks scheduled for you and all three leave events); the rest lands in the daily digest at
08:00 on your organisation's own clock (*Instellingen → Huisstijl → Tijdzone*, Settings → Branding →
Timezone). E-mail is off for every event until somebody switches it on.

The **Algemeen** (General) section holds **Bijna verlopen (dagen)** (Due soon, in days; 3 by
default), **Stille uren vanaf** / **tot** (Quiet hours from / until), and the **E-mailschema**
(E-mail digest schedule): the **Tijd** (Time) and **Dag** (Day) your daily and weekly e-mail digests
leave.

<Aside type="caution">
Quiet hours are stored but not yet applied. The screen says so itself. Do not rely on nothing
arriving overnight.
</Aside>

## Connecting a channel of your own

<Steps>
1. Go to *Instellingen → Meldingen* → **Kanalen** (Channels) → **Kanaal toevoegen** (Add channel).
2. Give it a **Naam** (Name) and pick the kind.
3. Paste what the provider actually gives you; schakl. converts it for you.
4. Press **Testen** (Test). On success it reads "Testbericht afgeleverd"; otherwise you see the
   provider's own error.
5. Set the hour and the weekday this channel's digests leave, on the channel itself.
6. Go back to the matrix and set a delivery in the new column for the events you want sent there.
</Steps>

| Kind | What you paste |
| --- | --- |
| E-mail | an e-mail address; sent through your organisation's e-mail transport |
| Slack | the URL of an Incoming Webhook for that channel |
| Microsoft Teams | the URL of an Incoming Webhook connector on that channel |
| Google Chat | the space's webhook URL |
| Discord | the webhook URL from channel settings → Integrations |
| Telegram | a **Bot-token** from @BotFather and the **Chat-ID** |
| Webhook | an http(s) URL; every notification is POSTed there as JSON |
| E-mail (Apprise) / Overig (Apprise-URL) | a bare Apprise URL, for anything else Apprise supports |

A freshly connected channel stays silent until you route it in the matrix. That is deliberate:
connecting a channel must not start buzzing somebody's phone on its own.

## Setting it for the whole organisation

*Instellingen → Standaardmeldingen* carries the same matrix, but as the default everyone inherits
until they change it themselves. It is also where you connect the **shared channels**: the team's
Slack or Teams channel, a group mailbox, a webhook into your own flow. Each gets its own column, so
"#crm gets new clients immediately and everything else once a day" is simply something you set.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it opens | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `notifications.notification.read` | The bell, the list and reading your own preferences | Everyone |
| `notifications.notification.write` | Marking read and saving your own preferences | Everyone |
| `notifications.channels.manage_own` | Connecting your own channels | Administrator and Member |
| `notifications.defaults.manage` | *Instellingen → Standaardmeldingen* | Administrator |
| `notifications.channels.manage` | The organisation's shared channels | Administrator |

Routing a shared channel takes both admin permissions at once: what lands in the team channel is a
decision the organisation makes, not whoever last opened their own settings.

## Good to know

- Credentials and webhook URLs are stored encrypted and never shown again; you only ever see a
  redacted preview. A webhook aimed at a private or local address is refused.
- If a delivery fails, the whole bundle stays pending with the provider's own error recorded on it,
  and is retried at growing intervals. Half a digest sent is not a state that exists.
- The **E-mail** kind rides your organisation's e-mail transport (*Instellingen → E-mail*), so it
  does nothing until that is configured.
- Resolving the underlying item resolves the notification: a decided leave request or a reviewed
  e-mail stops asking, for everyone who never opened it.
- Notifications are part of the core and need no licence key. There is no SMS, no push notification
  and no snooze; SMS exists only as a second factor at sign-in.

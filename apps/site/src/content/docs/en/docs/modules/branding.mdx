schakl. carries your agency's name, not ours. One screen holds the brand name, logo, favicon,
app icon and colours, and beside them the language, timezone, currency and country of your
organisation. Everything applies from the next page load, for everyone in the workspace; nothing
is rebuilt and nothing restarts.

## Where to find it

*Instellingen → Huisstijl* (Settings → Branding), in the group **Werkruimte** (Workspace). The
"Zoek een instelling" (Find a setting) box also finds it on words like "logo", "favicon",
"kleur", "tijdzone" or "valuta". It is one form with one **Opslaan** (Save) and two named
halves: **Merk** (Brand) and **Regio & formaat** (Region & format). A **Voorbeeld** (Preview)
panel beside the form follows along while you pick colours.

## Merk (Brand)

- **Merknaam** (Brand name, required). It appears at the top of the sidebar, on the login
  screen, in the browser tab title, as the name of the installed app, in the header of every
  outgoing e-mail, and on your invoices, quotes and reports wherever there is no logo.
- **Merknaam naast het logo tonen** (Show the brand name next to the logo). Leave it off and the
  sidebar carries the logo alone, and the tab title drops the brand prefix.
- **Titel browsertabblad** (Browser tab title). A template with `{page}` (the page name,
  required) and optionally `{brand}`. A live preview sits under the field, and the screen tells
  you a template is wrong before you save. Leave it empty for the default: *Brand · Page*.
- **Logo**. Shown in the sidebar, on the login screen, in the header of outgoing mail and on the
  PDF documents schakl. generates.
- **Favicon**. The browser tab icon.
- **App-icoon** (App icon). A square image of at least 512×512 px (PNG, JPEG or WebP). This
  becomes the home-screen icon when somebody installs the app; schakl. derives the 192 and 512
  sizes itself, including the "maskable" variant Android crops to a circle. The favicon stays
  the tab icon.
- **Primaire kleur** (Primary colour) and **Accentkleur** (Accent colour). The primary colour is
  what the app paints with: buttons, links, the active menu item, badges, and the browser's own
  checkboxes and date pickers. The same colour is the theme and splash colour of the installed
  app, the colour of the brand name in the e-mail header, and the fallback colour of your
  documents.

The three image fields share one picker: a thumbnail with **Bestand kiezen** (Choose file) or
**Vervangen** (Replace), and **Verwijderen** (Remove). To point at an asset you already host,
open **Een gehoste URL gebruiken** (Point at a hosted URL instead) and paste the URL.

## Regio & formaat (Region & format)

- **Standaardtaal** (Default language): Dutch or English, for everyone who has not chosen for
  themselves.
- **Tijdzone** (Timezone): event times are shown in this zone for everyone in the workspace.
- **Land** (Country): where the organisation operates. Used to read a phone number written
  without a country code, and as the default for a new client.
- **Valuta** (Currency): all amounts are shown in this currency for everyone.

## Where your branding turns up again

| Where | What it takes over |
| --- | --- |
| Login screen | Logo and brand name, before anyone is signed in |
| Outgoing e-mail | Logo and brand name in the primary colour, wrapped around every mail the platform sends |
| Invoices, quotes and reports | Logo, brand name and the primary colour as a fallback |
| Installed app (PWA) | Name, app icon, theme and background colour |
| Client portal | The same workspace, so the same branding |

## Custom domain

At the bottom of the screen sits an **Eigen domein** (Custom domain) card with its current
status. Setting it up happens in its own guided wizard (*Instellingen → Eigen domein*): prove
ownership with a TXT record first, then DNS and the TLS certificate. That is deliberately a
separate screen rather than a field on this form.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it opens | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `settings.branding.write` | *Instellingen → Huisstijl* and *Instellingen → Modules* | Administrator |
| `settings.domain.write` | *Instellingen → Eigen domein* | Administrator |

An owner holds everything. An administrator can also hand the key out on its own: somebody with
only `settings.branding.write` sees exactly those two cards in Instellingen and nothing else.
Uploading an image also needs the ordinary upload permission (`files.file.write`), and only
images are accepted here.

## Good to know

<Aside type="caution">
What you upload here is publicly readable. The logo, favicon and app icon must render on the
login screen before a session exists, so they are served without a login on your organisation's
address. Do not put anything here that must not leave the building.
</Aside>

- Changes apply from the next page load, for everyone. Nobody has to sign out.
- In dark mode your brand colours are lightened automatically if their contrast against the dark
  background would be too low. The hue is preserved, so your colour stays recognisable and you
  never have to supply a second set.
- The first-run wizard at `/setup` already asks for the brand name, colours and language. This
  screen is where you change them afterwards.
- The background image and accent colour of your invoice and quote template are not here but
  under *Instellingen → Facturatie* (Settings → Invoicing). That template falls back to your
  primary colour as long as you leave its own accent empty.
- A client's logo is a different thing from your own branding. You set it on the client's own
  page, and it is shown there and on that client's portal dashboard.

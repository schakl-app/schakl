Contacts are the people at your clients — the account owner, the marketer, the webmaster. Your
own colleagues are not among them; you manage those under *Instellingen → Team & gebruikers*
(Settings → Team & users). One person appears once in the address book and can be attached to as
many clients as needed.

![Contacts across every client, each showing the client they are attached to.](/img/docs/en-contacts.png)

## Where to find it

- Main menu **Contactpersonen** (Contacts): the whole address book.
- The **Contactpersonen** panel on every client page: the people of that one client.
- *Instellingen → Contactsoorten* (Settings → Contact types): the kinds of contact you
  distinguish.

## The list

The list is grouped by client, alphabetically, with **Zonder klant** (No client) last for people
attached to nobody. Someone who belongs to two clients is listed under both, so you find a person
where you would look for them rather than where the system filed them. The client sections count
only the contacts on the page you are viewing, and the list says so.

Above the list sit the search box and *Filter op klant* (Filter by client); as soon as you have
contact types, a row of chips appears to filter by type. On the right are the column picker,
Export and Import, and the ✎ that switches the selection checkboxes on. A client chip in a row is
brand-coloured when this person is the **primary** contact there.

## Recording a contact

Fields: **Voornaam** (First name, required), **Achternaam** (Last name), **E-mailadres**
(Email), **Telefoon** (Phone, with a country picker — a national number is read in your
organisation's country), **Functie** (Job title) and **Notities** (Notes). Notes are rich text
where `@` mentions a colleague or contact and `#` refers to a task. Custom fields you defined for
contacts under *Instellingen → Aangepaste velden* (Settings → Custom fields) appear below them,
and as columns in the list.

An email address already held by another contact is refused: "Er bestaat al een contactpersoon
met dit e-mailadres." Two cards for the same person are worse than one error message.

## Attaching to clients

The top of the contact page carries the **Klanten** (Clients) block, one chip per client.
Attaching, detaching and promoting to primary live in edit mode: ⋯ → *Bewerken* (Edit). The
*Klant toevoegen…* (Add client…) field searches existing clients and, on an unknown name, offers
the full **Nieuwe klant** (New client) form; once saved, the new client is attached straight away.

From the client's side you do the same in the **Contactpersonen** panel, which has its own
⋯ → *Bewerken*. Its search field queries the whole address book as you type, and a name that does
not exist yet opens the full create form and attaches the person in one step.

A client's first contact automatically becomes the primary one. A later link never quietly
displaces it; you switch it yourself, with *Als primair instellen* (Make primary).

## Contact types

Under *Instellingen → Contactsoorten* you manage the kinds of contact somebody can be for a
client: client contact, technical contact, billing, whatever you distinguish. Each type carries a
Dutch and an English label, an ordering and an active flag. Deleting a type leaves the links
intact — they simply carry no type any more.

The type belongs to the **link** between person and client, not to the person: the same man can
be the client contact at Acme and the technical contact at Beta.

<Aside type="caution">
The chip row above the list filters on contact type, but there is no field in the interface today
that assigns a type to a link; that can only be done through the API. Set your types up by all
means, but do not rely on them yet as a way of organising the address book.
</Aside>

## Several at once

The ✎ in the toolbar switches the checkboxes on and puts the actions beside itself. For contacts
there are two: attach a selection **to a client**, and **delete** a selection. Detaching is
deliberately not offered here; you do that on the person, where you can see which link you are
breaking. Deleting removes the person from *every* client they are attached to.

## Import & export

Contacts travel as **Contactpersonen**, through the buttons beside the column picker. The file
upserts on **email address**; a row without one always creates someone new. Columns:
`first_name` (required), `last_name`, `email`, `phone`, `job_title`, `notes` and `company` (exact
client name or id), plus your own custom fields. An empty `company` cell never detaches anyone.

A client list may carry the contact person in the same row: `contact_first_name`,
`contact_last_name`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone` and `contact_job_title` belong to the
**Klanten** (Clients) import. Those columns only appear for someone who may write contacts, and
an empty cell never wipes anything. See [Import & export](/en/docs/modules/import-export/).

## Client portal

If you run the client portal module, the contact page carries the **Klantportaal** (Client
portal) card: *Uitnodigen voor portaal* (Invite to portal), *Toegang uitschakelen* (Disable
access) and *Inloggen als deze contactpersoon* (Sign in as this contact). The last one shows you
the portal exactly as the client sees it, for half an hour. It is recorded on this contact's
activity trail, and everything you do meanwhile carries your name alongside.

## Permissions

Set per role under *Instellingen → Rollen* (Settings → Roles). By default:

| Permission | What it does | Held by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `contacts.contact.read` | View contacts | Administrator, Member, Client |
| `contacts.contact.write` | Create and edit | Administrator |
| `contacts.contact.delete` | Delete | Administrator |
| `contacts.link.write` | Attach to clients | Administrator |
| `contacts.type.read` | View contact types | Administrator, Member, Client |
| `contacts.type.manage` | Manage contact types | Administrator |

So a **Member** may only read by default. If you want your team creating contacts, tick
`contacts.contact.write` and `contacts.link.write` on that role. Export and import are separate
permissions (`impex.export`, `impex.import`) and default to Admin only.

## Good to know

- The bottom of the contact page carries the **activity trail**: who changed what, when. If the
  colleague who did it leaves, their name stays on the trail.
- A login holding the Client role only ever sees the contacts of its own companies; the whole
  address book is out of reach for them.
- A portal login attached to no company at all cannot add a contact — there would be no company
  to attach them to, and the message says exactly that.

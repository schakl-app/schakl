Time is where you record where your hours went, and where the agency sees which of them are
billable. An entry belongs to a client, a project and optionally a task; from there on project
budgets, retainers and invoices all count with it.

![Time tracking: the weekly grid, the timer, and what still needs approving.](/img/docs/en-time.png)

## Where to find it

**Uren** (Time) in the main menu is your own week. The team-wide side lives under **Overzicht**
(Overview): the tabs *Uren* (the hours overview), *Productiviteit* (Productivity) and *Omzet*
(Revenue). The client page and the project page each carry an **Uren** panel. The only settings
screen is *Instellingen → Uren-typen* (Settings → Time entry types).

## The timer

Top right sit **Omschrijving** (Description), **Klant** (Client), **Project** and the **▶
Starten** (Start) button. A running timer stays visible with a ticking counter wherever you go in
the app; **Stoppen** (Stop) writes the entry away. Only one runs per person: starting a new timer
automatically stops and saves the one already running.

## Logging an entry by hand

Beside the day sits the **Nieuwe registratie** (New entry) form, with **Van**, **Tot** and
**Pauze** (From, To and Break, in minutes) plus **Datum**, **Klant**, **Project**, **Taak**,
**Omschrijving** and **Type**. The **Duur** (Duration) field understands `1:30`, `90m` or `1,5`
and stays in sync with the times: change **Tot** and the duration follows, type a duration and
**Tot** moves. If **Tot** is at or before **Van**, the entry rolls into the next day, so work
crossing midnight need not be split in two.

Pick a project and **Beschikbare uren** (Hours available) appears underneath: spent against budget
for this period, with a bar and what is left. Where that budget comes from a retainer, the
agreement is named beside it.

Half-finished input stays put. The form autosaves a **Concept** (Draft) per day, shown as a chip
above the form and as an amber dot on the day tab; **Verwerpen** (Discard) throws it away. A draft
counts nowhere: not in totals, not in approval, not in invoicing.

### Billable follows the project

A new entry inherits **Standaard facturabel** (Billable by default) from the project you pick. A
project covered by an active subscription with included hours starts on **Niet facturabel**,
because the retainer already pays for those hours. Touch the toggle yourself and the form follows
your choice; switching project later no longer overrules it.

## The weekly timesheet

Below the day sits the **Weekstaat** (Weekly timesheet): one row per client / project / task
combination, with day totals and a week total. At the top you choose **Volledige week** (Full
week) or **Werkweek (ma–vr)** (Work week, Mon–Fri); that choice is saved as a personal preference.
Approved leave gets its own **Verlof** row and never counts toward the worked totals, because
leave is never written as a time entry.

## Quick add and dictation

When the **Urenhulp** (Time assist) feature is on under *Instellingen → AI*, a **Snel toevoegen**
(Quick add) field appears above the day: type `gisteren 14:00-16:30 website Jansen, homepage
overleg` into it and the form is filled in. Beside it sits **Vul mijn dag aan** (Fill in my day),
which suggests entries for the gap between your work schedule and what is already logged. If
**Spraak naar tekst** (Speech to text) is configured as well, you can dictate the line with the
microphone button. In every case the AI only fills in the form; nothing is saved until you click
**Opslaan** (Save).

## Approving and invoicing

*Overzicht → Uren* is the team list. You filter by employee, client, project, period and type, and
with the status pills on **Goed te keuren** (To approve), **Goedgekeurd** (Approved), **Te
factureren** (To invoice) and **Gefactureerd** (Invoiced). The totals at the bottom cover the whole
filtered set, not just the page on screen.

Tick rows and use **Goedkeuren** (Approve), **Goedkeuring ongedaan maken** (Unapprove) or
**Markeren als gefactureerd** (Mark as invoiced). Invoicing implies sign-off: a row you mark as
invoiced is approved at the same moment, and unapproving an invoiced row takes the invoiced flag
off with it.

<Aside type="caution">
An approved entry is locked for anyone without the *Approve hours* permission. You get the message
"Goedgekeurde uren zijn vergrendeld"; ask an admin to make the correction.
</Aside>

## Reports

*Overzicht → Productiviteit* puts hours, billable share and active days side by side per employee.
*Overzicht → Omzet* shows revenue per month, this year against last year, and the largest clients.
Billable hours are priced there at the **hourly rate of the employee who logged them**: the
*Uurtarief* field on the person under *Instellingen → Team & gebruikers* (Settings → Team &
users), otherwise the *Standaard uurtarief* under *Instellingen → Verlof* (Settings → Leave).
Hours logged by someone with no rate anywhere are left out of the money figure.

## Setting up time entry types

<Steps>
1. Go to *Instellingen → Uren-typen*.
2. Click **Nieuw type** (New type) and enter a Dutch and an English label. The key is derived from
   the label and never changes afterwards.
3. Save. The type appears as the **Type** field on the entry form and as a filter on the hours
   overview.
</Steps>

**Werk** (Work) and **E-mail** ship as defaults. A type already in use cannot be deleted;
deactivate it instead, and it leaves the picker while the history stays correct.

## Permissions

Set per role under *Instellingen → Rollen* (Settings → Roles).

| Permission | What it grants | Held by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `time.entry.read` | View time entries | Administrator (everyone's), Member (own only) |
| `time.entry.write` | Log and edit time | Administrator (everyone's), Member (own only) |
| `time.entry.approve` | Approve hours, and edit approved ones | Administrator |
| `time.entry.invoice` | Mark hours invoiced | Administrator |
| `time.report.read` | View the hours overview and reports | Administrator |
| `time.entry_type.read` | View time entry types | Administrator, Member |
| `time.entry_type.manage` | Manage time entry types | Administrator |

The Owner role holds everything. Import and export additionally require `impex.export` and
`impex.import`, which default to Admin only.

## Good to know

- **Export and import sit on the Uren screen itself**, scoped to the week you are looking at. An
  import creates entries on your own timesheet; approval and invoicing are export-only columns.
  See [Import & export](/en/docs/modules/import-export/).
- **A reminder goes out on Monday morning** to anyone who logged nothing the previous week. People
  who had approved leave for that whole week are skipped.
- **A draft untouched for 30 days is cleaned up.**
- **You can log time straight from an interaction** with the "Voeg aan mijn uren toe" checkbox; the
  client, project and subject travel with it.
- Time is a licensed module: when the licence lapses everything stays readable and exportable, but
  writing stops.

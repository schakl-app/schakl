A meeting with a client produces agreements, and agreements nobody writes down are gone within a
week. With Meetings you record the meeting; your organisation's speech provider transcribes it,
your own AI drafts the minutes with the decisions and action items, and you review them. Only
when you confirm do the minutes land on the client as a contact moment and the ticked action
items become tasks.

## Where to find it

- Main menu **Vergaderingen** (Meetings): every recorded meeting, newest first, with search across
  titles and transcripts and a client filter. From here you start **Vergadering opnemen** (Record
  a meeting).
- The **Vergaderingen** panel on the client page, with the number of action items per meeting.
- *Instellingen → Vergaderingen*: the consent statement, the minutes document and your house
  rules for the minutes.
- *Instellingen → AI*: the **Vergadertranscripten en verslagen** (Meeting transcripts and minutes)
  feature and the speech provider.

<Aside type="caution">
Meetings is a licensed module. Switch it on under *Instellingen → Modules* and make sure your
licence covers it. Without cover the module goes read-only after the grace period: the minutes and
transcripts stay readable, but nothing new is recorded.
</Aside>

## Before you start

Recording needs two things under *Instellingen → AI*, and without both schakl. draws no record
button, only a notice saying what is missing:

- The **Vergadertranscripten en verslagen** feature is on. It is a switch of its own, separate from
  the other AI features: a meeting is mostly *other people's* words, and they go to a model whole.
- A **Spraakaanbieder** (speech provider) that can transcribe is set up, on its own key. For
  meetings, pick a model that tells speakers apart: **Mistral (Voxtral)** with
  `voxtral-mini-latest` (three hours in one request) or OpenAI `gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize` (25
  minutes per request). `gpt-4o-transcribe`, `gpt-transcribe` and `whisper-1` return the words
  only; the recorder says so above the record button, so you do not find out afterwards.

A recording longer than the provider accepts in one request is cut into parts and transcribed part
by part. A speaker may then carry a different label per part (S1, S2 · S3, S4); the review screen
says how many parts the recording was transcribed in.

## Recording a meeting

<Steps>

1. Open **Vergaderingen** and click **Vergadering opnemen** (Record a meeting).
2. Fill in the **Titel** (Title), the **Klant** (Client — action items become this client's tasks)
   and optionally the **Project**.
3. Fill in **Wie zit er aan tafel** (Who is at the table): colleagues, the client's contacts, or a
   name under *Anders* (Other). You are added yourself. The minutes put every action item on the
   name of whoever took it on.
4. Choose the **Soort** (Kind: *Op locatie* / In person, or *Online*) and **Wat wordt opgenomen**
   (What to record): *Microfoon* for a meeting in the room, *Browsertabblad* (Browser tab) for a
   Meet, Teams or Zoom call in this browser (plus your microphone), or *Bestand uploaden* (Upload a
   file) for a recording you already have.
5. Tick *Ik heb iedereen in deze vergadering verteld dat er wordt opgenomen* (I have told everyone
   in this meeting that it is being recorded). Underneath is what to say: why (the minutes), who
   reads it (your team) and how long the audio is kept.
6. Click **Opname starten** (Start recording). The screen shows the clock and *Opgeslagen tot …*
   (Saved up to …). Done? Click **Stoppen en uitschrijven** (Stop and transcribe).

</Steps>

While recording, the browser sends a piece to the server every minute (the first after only a few
seconds). A crashed tab therefore costs a minute at most, not the meeting. If the server refuses a
piece because the connection drops for a moment or schakl. is being updated, the browser retries
for up to ten minutes while the recording carries on; the screen then says *Verbinding
herstellen…* (Reconnecting…) in amber. Only if that fails does the line turn red with **Opnieuw
opslaan** (Try saving again). If you try to leave the page while a recording runs, schakl. asks first.

<Aside type="tip">
Keep the screen open and awake. A locked phone freezes the tab and stops the recording; whatever
was saved is kept. When a recording stops that way, the meeting page offers **Verwerk wat is
opgeslagen** (Process what was saved), and if nobody does, schakl. finishes it on its own within
half an hour. If nothing arrived at all, whoever started the recording is told, by mail too.
</Aside>

After that the meeting moves through *In de wachtrij* (Queued), *Wordt uitgeschreven*
(Transcribing) and *Verslag wordt geschreven* (Writing minutes). A long meeting takes a few
minutes; you can leave the page. When the minutes are ready you get a notification, by default by
mail too: *The minutes of a meeting I recorded are ready*. If something goes wrong the meeting
shows *Mislukt* (Failed) with the reason and an **Opnieuw proberen** (Try again) button.

## Review and confirm

A meeting on **Na te kijken** (To review) shows the draft beside the transcript and the recording.
Every text field is formatted text you can also paste an image into:

- **Samenvatting** (Summary): a short account of the meeting.
- **Besproken** (Discussed): one field with a heading per topic and what was said about it
  underneath.
- **Besluiten** (Decisions), **Actiepunten** (Action items) and **Open vragen** (Open questions),
  each with a button to add one.
- Under every decision and action item is the quote from the transcript it rests on, with the
  time. If that quote is not in the transcript, the item stays with the label *Niet in het
  transcript gevonden* (Not found in the transcript). Read such items before you confirm; the
  count sits next to the button.

Action items are grouped by side and by person: **Voor ons** (For us to do) under each colleague,
**Voor de klant** (For the client to do) under each contact, and **Overig** (Others). Per item you
choose *Wie pakt het op* (Who took it on), a **Deadline** (Due) and a **Toelichting** (Details), and the
*Als taak aanmaken* (Create as a task) tick. A promise by the client is minuted, not put on your
board — unless you tick it, which makes it a task for that contact (waiting on the client).

Next to the transcript you pair the participants with their speaker label (S1, S2 …) and click
**Deelnemers opslaan** (Save participants). The usual case is doing that after the first draft;
**Verslag opnieuw opstellen** (Write the minutes again) then redrafts over the same transcript, with
the people known, without transcribing again and so without new audio cost.

The **Deze vergadering in woorden aanpassen** (Change this meeting in words) box changes the
meeting in your own words, for example *set the client to Nova, S2 is Jan Jansen, add an action
item for Sanne: send the logo, due Friday*. It changes the title, client, project, kind and date,
who was there and which speaker label they are, and while the minutes are under review every line
of them. Everything is applied in your name, like an ordinary edit.

**Concept opslaan** (Save draft) keeps your work; **Verslag bevestigen** (Confirm minutes) makes it
a record:

- A contact moment is filed on the client (*Afspraak op locatie* or *Online afspraak*), with the
  contacts who were there.
- Every ticked action item becomes a task. Tasks and contact moments go through their own
  modules, with their own rules and permissions; an action item that could not become a task is
  named with the reason after confirming, rather than blocking the whole minutes.

Confirmed minutes show the *Contactmoment openen* (Open the contact moment) link and the tasks created.

## Downloading

**Downloaden** (Download) opens *Notulen downloaden* (Download the minutes). Tick which chapters to
include: who was there, summary, discussed, decisions, action items, open questions, the evidence
under decisions and action items (quote and time), and the full transcript as an appendix. The
transcript is off unless you tick it. **Voorbeeld openen** (Open preview) shows exactly the page
**Pdf downloaden** (Download PDF) prints.

In the PDF the attendees are grouped under your agency's name and the client's, with a profile
picture or initials. Action items sit per side under the person who took them on, and an item not
found in the transcript stays marked on paper too.

Under *Transcript als bestand* (Transcript as a file) you download just the words, with speakers
and times: as **Tekst (.txt)**, **Markdown (.md)** or subtitles (**.srt**, **.vtt**) for a video
editor.

The recording plays on a phone; where a browser cannot play the format (Safari plays no WebM),
there is a link to download the file instead.

## Instellingen → Vergaderingen

For administrators, under *Instellingen → Vergaderingen*:

- **Opnemen** (Recording): *Vraag om de toestemmingsverklaring voordat een opname begint* (Ask for
  the consent statement before a recording starts), on by default. If your agency has its own
  procedure for this, switch it off; the tick and the check then go together.
- **Notulen geschreven door AI** (Minutes written by AI): **Huisregels voor de notulen** (House
  rules for the minutes), your own writing instruction (tone, length, what must always be in it).
  The model receives it as a style, never as licence to add what was not said.
- **Het notulendocument** (The minutes document): the **Ontwerp** (Design: *Standaard* or *Eigen
  HTML* — Jinja and CSS in a sandbox without network access), the **Accentkleur** (Accent colour),
  a **Kopafbeelding** (Header image), an **Afsluitende regel** (Closing line), profile pictures
  beside names on or off, and the **Standaard aangevinkte hoofdstukken** (Chapters ticked by
  default). The preview beside it draws your most recent minutes with these settings.

## Permissions

Configurable per role under *Instellingen → Rollen*. Defaults:

| Permission | What it does | Default for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `meetings.meeting.read` | View meetings and minutes | Admin, Member |
| `meetings.meeting.write` | Record meetings and review minutes | Admin, Member |
| `meetings.meeting.delete` | Delete meetings | Admin |
| `meetings.settings.manage` | Manage meeting settings | Admin |

What confirming produces also needs those modules' permissions: filing a contact moment and
creating tasks. Someone who may review minutes but not create tasks is told so per action item.

## Privacy and retention

- **The audio is deleted 30 days after confirming**; the transcript and the minutes stay. Whoever
  may review minutes can delete the audio sooner with **Audio verwijderen** (Delete the audio).
- **A recording can be played by whoever may see the meeting**, and by nobody else who happens to
  be able to point at the file.
- **A client never sees a meeting.** A client portal login gets an empty list, no panel and no
  recording or transcript, even if someone grants the Client role the read permission. What the
  client is owed is the confirmed contact moment, under the rules of
  [Interactions](/en/docs/modules/interactions/).
- A member restricted to a client group sees only those clients' meetings.
- Deleting a meeting removes the audio, the transcript and the draft minutes; a contact moment and
  tasks already confirmed from it stay.

## API and MCP

Everything the screen does goes through the API under `/api/v1/meetings`, and so is also a tool on
the [MCP server](/en/docs/integrations/mcp/): create a meeting, post the recording in pieces
(base64 in JSON, so an AI client can do it too), finish, set participants, redraft, confirm, fetch
the PDF and the transcript. The transcript comes as JSON by default, with the time and the
speaker's name per line; `format=txt|md|srt|vtt` returns a file.

For the assistant there are three read tools: `meetings.find`, `meetings.transcript` and
`meetings.minutes`, all three behind `meetings.meeting.read`.

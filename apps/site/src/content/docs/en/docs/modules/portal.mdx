The client portal gives a client's contact person their own login into your own app, under your own
branding. That person sees the marketing overview you composed for them, their own company's
invoices and the reports you published. Everything else stays invisible — not because somebody
remembered to tick a box, but because the link to their company is what decides what they can ask
for at all.

## Where to find it

There is no settings screen of its own. The button sits on the person: *Contactpersonen* (Contacts) →
open the contact → the **Klantportaal** (Client portal) card. Under *Instellingen → Modules*
(Settings → Modules) the module is called "Klantportaal".

## Inviting someone

<Steps>
1. Make sure the contact is linked to at least one client. That link is exactly what they will be
   able to see.
2. Make sure they have an e-mail address of their own. Without one you cannot invite: "Deze
   contactpersoon heeft een e-mailadres nodig voordat je kunt uitnodigen."
3. Press **Uitnodigen voor portaal** (Invite to portal). An account is created, given the *Klant*
   (Client) role, linked to this contact, and a mail in your own branding goes out with a link to
   set a password.
4. From then on the card shows a status: **Geen toegang** (No access), **Uitgenodigd** (Invited),
   **Actief** (Active) or **Uitgeschakeld** (Disabled). While the status is "Uitgenodigd" a
   **Uitnodiging opnieuw versturen** (Resend invite) button appears too.
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
The invitation mail rides your organisation's e-mail transport (*Instellingen → E-mail*). If that is
not configured yet, the access is still created but the mail is not sent, and the card says so in as
many words. Configure e-mail, then resend the invitation.

An e-mail address that already belongs to an existing account (a colleague's, or an account in
another organisation) is refused. A portal login needs an address of its own.
</Aside>

## What the client sees

The client signs in at the same web address, in the same branding, and lands on **Jouw dashboard**
(Your dashboard): a widget board they arrange themselves. With more than one company they get a
company switcher at the top, showing the selected company's own logo.

- **Marketing**: the overview your agency composed for them. They may move or drop tiles, but never
  add anything you did not expose.
- **Facturen** (Invoices): only their own companies' invoices, with status and the same PDF you send
  out. An invoice with status *Concept* (Draft) is never visible to them.
- **Rapportages** (Reports): published reports only.
- **Taken** (Tasks): commenting on their own tasks.

That is the whole write surface: comments on their own tasks, the layout of their own dashboard and
menu, and their own notifications. Nothing else.

## Switching access off again

**Toegang uitschakelen** (Disable access) switches the login off without throwing anything away: the
account, the link and the history all survive, and the button then reads **Toegang opnieuw
inschakelen** (Re-enable access). There is deliberately no button that deletes a portal login.

## Signing in as this contact

When a client says "I can't find the report", you do not have to guess. The same card carries
**Inloggen als deze contactpersoon** (Sign in as this contact). You confirm first, then land on the
client's own dashboard for half an hour.

- Your own session stays open; it is not replaced.
- A banner sits on every screen: `{you} is ingelogd als {client}`, with a **Stoppen** (Stop) button.
- The session can never do more than you can yourself. If you are seeing less than the client does,
  the banner says so: "u ziet minder dan zij: alleen wat uw eigen rechten toelaten".
- The start and the end land on the contact's own activity trail ("logde in als …", "beëindigde de
  sessie als …"), and anything you change meanwhile carries your name beside the client's.

If you are already signed in as somebody else, you have to stop that first.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it opens | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `members.member.write` | The whole Klantportaal card: invite, resend, disable | Administrator |
| `portal.login.impersonate` | "Inloggen als de portaallogin van een klant" | Administrator |

Both are set per role under *Instellingen → Rollen* (Settings → Roles). Signing in as a client never
follows automatically from managing their login: those are two different acts. Somebody without
`members.member.write` does not see the card at all.

The invited client gets the shipped **Klant** (Client) role: read access to their own data, plus commenting
on their own tasks.

## Good to know

- The client portal is a **licensed module**. Without a valid licence, **Uitnodigen voor portaal**
  renders with a padlock and the badge "Niet inbegrepen" (Not included), opening a dialog that
  explains what to do on this deployment. Existing client logins keep working throughout: an expired
  licence makes the module read-only, not gone.
- A client login linked to no company sees nothing at all, whatever its role holds. *Instellingen →
  Team & gebruikers* (Settings → Team & users) therefore badges such an account **Ziet geen klanten**
  (Sees no clients).
- Contacts are the only kind of portal subject today.
- A contact outside your own client groups does not exist for you: you get "not found" rather than
  "no access".

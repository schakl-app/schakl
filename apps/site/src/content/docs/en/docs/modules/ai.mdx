schakl. runs no AI service of its own and sells no credits. You connect your own account at
Anthropic, OpenAI or a server that speaks the OpenAI API, and every AI feature in the platform
runs through that one key, that one budget and that one usage meter. With nothing configured, no
AI control is drawn anywhere in the app.

## Where to find it

- *Instellingen → AI* (Settings → AI), in the **Communicatie & koppelingen** (Communication &
  integrations) group: provider, key, model, features, house style, budget, speech and usage.
- The **Assistent** (Assistant): the sparkle icon at the top right of the bar, on every page.
- The **Schrijfhulp** (Writing assist): the sparkle icon in the toolbar of every rich text field.
- The **Urenhulp** (Time assist): the **Snel toevoegen** (Quick add) field above the entry form on
  *Uren* (Hours).
- **Brief me** and **AI-rapport** (AI report): two buttons on the client page.

## Setting it up

<Steps>
1. Open *Instellingen → AI*. You need the **AI-instellingen beheren** (Manage AI settings)
   permission, which admins hold by default.

2. Pick a **Provider**: Anthropic, OpenAI or **OpenAI-compatibel (eigen server)**
   (OpenAI-compatible, own server). The last one is anything speaking the OpenAI chat API at an
   address of your own.

3. Paste your key into **API-sleutel** (API key). It is stored encrypted and never played back:
   next time it reads "Er is een sleutel opgeslagen; laat leeg om die te behouden" (a key is
   stored; leave empty to keep it), and leaving it blank keeps it.

4. Fill in **Basis-URL** (Base URL) only for OpenAI-compatible, for example
   `https://ai.example.com/v1`. Saving without it is a validation error on that field.

5. Leave **Model** empty for the provider's default, or type one yourself. The field is free text
   so a new model needs no new release of schakl.; **Modellen ophalen** (Fetch models) pulls your
   provider's current list in as suggestions.

6. Tick what you want under **Functies** (Features), optionally fill in **Huisstijl** (House
   style) and **Maandelijks tokenbudget** (Monthly token budget), and press **Opslaan** (Save).

7. Press **Verbinding testen** (Test connection). It round-trips a small request through the
   stored settings; on a failure the provider's own error text is shown verbatim.
</Steps>

## The four features

Each feature is its own checkbox with an **Eigen model (optioneel)** (Model override) box beside
it, so you can put a fast model on the time assist and leave the larger one for writing.

**Assistent.** The sparkle icon in the top bar opens the **Assistent** slide-over, where you ask
about your own clients, projects, tasks and hours ("How many hours did I log this week?"). On a
client, project or task page it inherits that record, so "this client" resolves. Answers carry
source chips that deep-link to the records they came from. The assistant is deliberately
read-only: it looks things up and answers, it never creates or changes anything. And it only sees
what you may see.

**Schrijfhulp.** Every rich text field (client, contact, project, task, interaction, invoice and
quote texts, task and subscription templates, and long-text custom fields) carries a sparkle icon
with: Verbeteren (Improve), Inkorten (Shorten), Uitbreiden (Expand), Spelling & grammatica
(Spelling & grammar), Zakelijke toon (Business tone), Informele toon (Informal tone), Vertalen
naar Nederlands / Engels (Translate to Dutch / English) and Schrijf uit (Write out). The result
appears separately first, and you choose **Vervangen** (Replace) or **Invoegen** (Insert).
Whatever you put under **Huisstijl** (up to 4,000 characters, e.g. "write informally, address the
reader as je, keep sentences short") is prepended to every writing request.

**Urenhulp.** On *Uren* you type one line into **Snel toevoegen**, for example "gisteren
14:00-16:30 website Jansen, homepage overleg". The server looks up your own clients, projects and
tasks and prefills the entry form with them; saving is yours. **Vul mijn dag aan** (Fill in my
day) compares your work schedule with your approved leave and what you already logged, and
suggests draft rows for the gap from tasks you demonstrably worked on that day. Nothing there is
saved without your click either.

**Briefing & rapporten** (Digest & reports). On a client page, **Brief me** summarises that
client's recent activity, with source chips. **AI-rapport** drafts a monthly report you edit in
the editor and save as a record. This is the light version in the core; the full report with a
branded PDF, a schedule and sending is the [reporting module](/en/docs/modules/reporting/).

## Dictating instead of typing

Speech to text needs a credential of its own, because Anthropic (the default provider) has no
transcription service. *Instellingen → AI* carries a **Spraak naar tekst** (Speech to text) block
for it:

- **Spraakaanbieder** (Speech provider): *Uit* (Off), OpenAI or OpenAI-compatible. Anthropic is
  deliberately absent, because that setting could never work. On *Uit* your chat provider is
  reused, which only resolves if that is OpenAI or a compatible server.
- **API-sleutel voor spraak** (Speech API key), **Basis-URL** (compatible only), **Spraakmodel**
  (Speech model, empty means `whisper-1`) and **Maandbudget audio (seconden)** (Monthly audio
  budget in seconds). Audio is counted in seconds rather than tokens, so it gets its own budget.

A microphone then appears beside **Snel toevoegen** on *Uren*: **Inspreken** (Dictate), speak,
**Opname stoppen** (Stop recording), and the transcript lands in the field. Reading it back is
the whole point — Dutch proper nouns are exactly what a recogniser gets wrong, and "Janssen"
instead of "Jansen" silently matches no client at all.

The microphone is drawn only when all four hold: a speech service is configured, **Urenhulp** is
on, you may write time entries, and the browser is allowed to record. That last one requires
`https://`. A recording runs to at most 60 seconds; too long, or an unsupported format, is a
clean error rather than a half-transcribed line.

## Budget and usage

**Maandelijks tokenbudget** is a soft cap per calendar month; empty means unlimited. The
**Verbruik** (Usage) panel shows this month's requests and tokens per feature, with a bar that
turns amber at 80% and red at 100%. Over the cap, the app says "Het maandelijkse AI-budget is
bereikt." (the monthly AI budget has been reached) with a **Toch doorgaan** (Proceed anyway)
button, so an urgent question does not strand on a limit you set yourself.

Usage records counts and labels only. The text of a question or an answer is stored nowhere in
schakl.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it opens | Held by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ai.use` | Using the AI features: assistant, writing assist, time assist, briefings and dictation | Administrator, Member |
| `ai.settings.manage` | *Instellingen → AI*: provider, key, features, budget, test and usage | Administrator |
| `time.entry.write` | Additionally required to dictate, because the transcript becomes a time entry | Administrator, Member (own entries) |

Clients with a portal login are not given `ai.use`, on purpose: AI reads across the very records
a client role is scoped away from.

## Good to know

- **A feature you just toggled can take up to half a minute** to appear for colleagues: the list
  of active features is briefly cached.
- **The model name is free text**, so a typo is not a validation error but a provider error on
  first use. **Verbinding testen** brings that forward.
- **Relative dates in the time assist match your day**: they resolve in the organisation's
  timezone and against the day you are actually looking at, not the server's clock.
- **If your sentence says nothing about billable, the field stays empty** and the project's own
  setting wins. It is never quietly defaulted to non-billable.
- **Names are matched literally.** "Jansn" finds nothing. What you yourself logged in the last
  thirty days is weighed in, so Tuesday's client is usually recognised anyway.
- **Dictation works only in the Snel toevoegen field** on *Uren* today, nowhere else.
- **AI-configuratie verwijderen** (Remove AI configuration) at the bottom of the page takes every
  AI feature away until a new key is entered. The usage history is kept.
- **An expired licence stops generation**, while the settings screen and the usage history stay
  readable.

<Aside type="caution" title="Do not rotate the encryption key without re-entering the API key">
Provider keys are stored encrypted with the installation's encryption key
(`SCHAKL_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, falling back to `SCHAKL_SECRET_KEY`). If that changes and the keys are
not re-entered, the stored secret can no longer be decrypted and the organisation silently reads
as "AI is nog niet ingesteld" (AI is not set up yet). Paste the keys again in *Instellingen → AI*.
</Aside>

schakl. runs no AI service of its own and sells no credits. You connect your own account at
Anthropic, OpenAI or a server that speaks the OpenAI API, and every AI feature in the platform
runs through that one key, that one budget and that one usage meter. With nothing configured, no
AI control is drawn anywhere in the app.

## Where to find it

- *Instellingen → AI* (Settings → AI), in the **Integraties** (Communication &
  integrations) group: provider, key, model, features, house style, budget, speech and usage.
- The **Assistent** (Assistant): the sparkle icon at the top right of the bar, on every page.
- The **Schrijfhulp** (Writing assist): the sparkle icon in the toolbar of every rich text field.
- The **Urenhulp** (Time assist): the **Snel toevoegen** (Quick add) field above the entry form on
  *Uren* (Hours).
- **Brief me** and **AI-rapport** (AI report): two buttons on the client page.
- On *Taken* (Tasks): **Inspreken** (Dictate) beside **Nieuwe taak**, and on every task the
  **Aanpassen met schakl** box and the **Stappen schrijven met schakl** button.
- *Instellingen → Taken* (Settings → Tasks): the task address, whose blanks the AI fills in.

AI falls under the commercial licence; see [Licences](/en/docs/admin/licenses/).

## Setting it up

<Steps>
1. Open *Instellingen → AI*. You need the **AI-instellingen beheren** (Manage AI settings)
   permission, which admins hold by default.

2. Pick a **Provider**: Anthropic, OpenAI or **OpenAI-compatibel (eigen server)**
   (OpenAI-compatible, own server). The last one is anything speaking the OpenAI chat API at an
   address of your own.

3. Paste your key into **API-sleutel** (API key). It is stored encrypted and never played back:
   next time it reads "Er is een sleutel opgeslagen; laat leeg om die te behouden" (a key is
   stored; leave empty to keep it), and leaving it blank keeps it.

4. Fill in **Basis-URL** (Base URL) only for OpenAI-compatible, for example
   `https://ai.example.com/v1`. Saving without it is a validation error on that field.

5. Leave **Model** empty for the provider's default, or type one yourself. The field is free text
   so a new model needs no new release of schakl.; **Modellen ophalen** (Fetch models) pulls your
   provider's current list in as suggestions.

6. Tick what you want under **Functies** (Features), optionally fill in **Huisstijl** (House
   style) and **Maandelijks tokenbudget** (Monthly token budget), and press **Opslaan** (Save).

7. Press **Verbinding testen** (Test connection). It round-trips a small request through the
   stored settings; on a failure the provider's own error text is shown verbatim.
</Steps>

## The features

Each feature is its own checkbox with an **Eigen model (optioneel)** (Model override) box beside
it, so you can put a fast model on the time assist and leave the larger one for writing. Features
that read what somebody else wrote (an e-mail, a meeting) deliberately get a checkbox of their
own: an agency happy for AI to polish a colleague's paragraph has not thereby agreed to it reading
a client's mailbox.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Assistent** | The chat panel: questions about your whole workspace, and recording a stated list of things |
| **Schrijfhulp** (Writing assist) | Rewriting, shortening, translating and writing out in every rich text field |
| **Urenhulp** (Time assist) | Snel toevoegen and Vul mijn dag aan on *Uren* |
| **Briefing & rapporten** | Brief me and the AI report on the client page |
| **E-mail in taak** (E-mail into task) | Filling in a task you create while approving an e-mail, from that e-mail |
| **Taak inspreken, aanpassen en plannen** (Dictate, adjust and plan tasks) | Dictating a task, changing a task in words, writing its steps |
| **E-mail naar taakadres** (Mail to task address) | Filling in what a task mailed to the task address left open |
| **Vergadertranscripten en verslagen** (Meeting transcripts and minutes) | Transcribing a recorded meeting and drafting its minutes ([Meetings](/en/docs/modules/meetings/)) |

**Assistent.** The sparkle icon in the top bar opens the **Assistent** slide-over. You ask about
anything in your workspace (clients, projects, tasks, hours, domains, invoices, marketing
figures): the assistant searches the same API an external AI client gets over MCP, and so sees
exactly what you may see and nothing more. On a client, project or task page it inherits that
record, so "this client" resolves. Answers carry source chips that deep-link to the records they
came from, and while it works you see what it is reading ("Leest domeinen…", reading domains).

It writes only from a fixed list: **create or change a task**, **post a comment**, **log hours**
and **start or stop the timer**. Every write goes through, as you, the same checks as doing it on
the screen yourself, with your permissions and on your activity trail ("Opslaan…", saving).
Configuring something, sending an invoice or publishing a report is beyond it; widening that list
is a deliberate decision in the code, not a search result.

**Schrijfhulp.** Every rich text field (client, contact, project, task, interaction, invoice and
quote texts, task and subscription templates, and long-text custom fields) carries a sparkle icon
with: Verbeteren (Improve), Inkorten (Shorten), Uitbreiden (Expand), Spelling & grammatica
(Spelling & grammar), Zakelijke toon (Business tone), Informele toon (Informal tone), Vertalen
naar Nederlands / Engels (Translate to Dutch / English) and Schrijf uit (Write out). The result
appears separately first, and you choose **Vervangen** (Replace) or **Invoegen** (Insert).
Whatever you put under **Huisstijl** (up to 4,000 characters, e.g. "write informally, address the
reader as je, keep sentences short") is prepended to every writing request.

**Urenhulp.** On *Uren* you type one line into **Snel toevoegen**, for example "gisteren
14:00-16:30 website Jansen, homepage overleg". The server looks up your own clients, projects and
tasks and prefills the entry form with them; saving is yours. Whatever the line itself says
unambiguously ("gisteren", "afgelopen vrijdag", 14:00-16:30, "2 uur", "90 min") is filled in even
without the model, so half a match still yields half a line. **Vul mijn dag aan** (Fill in my
day) compares your work schedule with your approved leave and what you already logged, and
suggests draft rows for the gap from tasks you demonstrably worked on that day. Nothing there is
saved without your click either.

**Briefing & rapporten** (Digest & reports). On a client page, **Brief me** summarises that
client's recent activity, with source chips. **AI-rapport** drafts a monthly report you edit in
the editor and save as a record. This is the light version in the core; the full report with a
branded PDF, a schedule and sending is the [reporting module](/en/docs/modules/reporting/).

**Tasks.** Three features work on [Tasks](/en/docs/modules/tasks/):

- **Taak inspreken, aanpassen en plannen.** **Inspreken** lets you say a task in one go and hands
  you a draft you read through whole before anything is saved. On an existing task you type or
  speak what should change into the **Aanpassen met schakl** box; schakl. applies it as you, under
  your name on the trail. **Stappen schrijven met schakl** builds a checklist from the title and
  the description.
- **E-mail in taak.** Create a task while approving an e-mail and schakl. reads that e-mail and
  fills the task in: description, steps, a deadline if there is none yet, the links from the
  mail. It writes from your agency's side: a mail you sent the client becomes a task for you, not
  for the client. Who gets the task, for which client and whether the client sees it are things
  an e-mail can never decide, however it is worded.
- **E-mail naar taakadres.** A mail to the task address becomes a task; the AI only fills in what
  your own words left open and never overwrites what you wrote.

## Dictating instead of typing

Speech to text needs a credential of its own, because Anthropic (the default provider) has no
transcription service. *Instellingen → AI* carries a **Spraak naar tekst** (Speech to text) block
for it:

- **Spraakaanbieder** (Speech provider): *Uit* (Off), OpenAI, OpenAI-compatible or Mistral
  (Voxtral). Anthropic is deliberately absent, because that setting could never work. On *Uit*
  your chat provider is reused, which only resolves if that is OpenAI or a compatible server.
- **API-sleutel voor spraak** (Speech API key), **Basis-URL** (compatible only), **Spraakmodel**
  (Speech model; empty means `whisper-1` on OpenAI) and **Maandbudget audio (seconden)** (Monthly
  audio budget in seconds). Audio is counted in seconds rather than tokens, so it gets its own
  budget. For meetings, pick a model that labels who spoke; the screen names which ones do.

There are four places with a microphone, and each follows its own feature's checkbox:

| Where | Feature | At most |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Snel toevoegen** on *Uren* | Urenhulp | 2 minutes |
| **Inspreken** on *Taken*, and the **Aanpassen met schakl** box on a task | Taak inspreken, aanpassen en plannen | 5 minutes |
| The **Assistent**'s input box | Assistent | 5 minutes |
| Recording a meeting | Vergadertranscripten en verslagen | as long as the meeting |

Whatever you dictate always lands as text in the field first. Reading it back is the whole
point — Dutch proper nouns are exactly what a recogniser gets wrong, and "Janssen" instead of
"Jansen" silently matches no client at all. In the assistant you send the message yourself; with
a dictated task you read the draft before anything is created.

The microphone is drawn only when a speech service is configured, that place's feature is on, you
may do the thing (log hours, create tasks) and the browser is allowed to record. That last one
requires `https://`. While recording, the counter runs with the cap beside it (`0:42 / 5:00`), and
when the cap is reached the recording stops and the screen says so. Too long, or an unsupported
format, is a clean error rather than a half-transcribed line.

## Budget and usage

**Maandelijks tokenbudget** is a soft cap per calendar month; empty means unlimited. The
**Verbruik** (Usage) panel shows this month's requests and tokens per feature, with a bar that
turns amber at 80% and red at 100%. Over the cap, the app says "Het maandelijkse AI-budget is
bereikt." (the monthly AI budget has been reached) with a **Toch doorgaan** (Proceed anyway)
button, so an urgent question does not strand on a limit you set yourself.

Usage records counts and labels only. The text of a question or an answer is stored nowhere in
schakl.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it opens | Held by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ai.use` | Using the AI features: assistant, writing assist, time assist, briefings, tasks and dictation | Administrator, Member |
| `ai.settings.manage` | *Instellingen → AI*: provider, key, features, budget, test and usage | Administrator |
| `time.entry.write` | Additionally required to dictate hours, because the transcript becomes a time entry | Administrator, Member (own entries) |
| `tasks.task.create` / `tasks.task.write` | Additionally required to dictate a task or change one with schakl | Administrator, Member |

Clients with a portal login are not given `ai.use`, on purpose: AI reads across the very records
a client role is scoped away from. What the assistant records additionally needs exactly the
permission the screen would ask for.

## Good to know

- **A feature you just toggled can take up to half a minute** to appear for colleagues: the list
  of active features is briefly cached.
- **The model name is free text**, so a typo is not a validation error but a provider error on
  first use. **Verbinding testen** brings that forward.
- **Relative dates in the time assist match your day**: they resolve in the organisation's
  timezone and against the day you are actually looking at, not the server's clock.
- **If your sentence says nothing about billable, the field stays empty** and the project's own
  setting wins. It is never quietly defaulted to non-billable.
- **Names are matched literally.** "Jansn" finds nothing. What you yourself logged in the last
  thirty days is weighed in, so Tuesday's client is usually recognised anyway.
- **An e-mail cannot make the AI do what it may not.** For *E-mail in taak*, assignee, client,
  project, status and client visibility are simply not among the fields the model may fill in,
  and a link only comes along if it appears verbatim in the mail.
- **AI-configuratie verwijderen** (Remove AI configuration) at the bottom of the page takes every
  AI feature away until a new key is entered. The usage history is kept.
- **An expired licence stops generation**, while the settings screen and the usage history stay
  readable.

<Aside type="caution" title="Do not rotate the encryption key without re-entering the API key">
Provider keys are stored encrypted with the installation's encryption key
(`SCHAKL_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, falling back to `SCHAKL_SECRET_KEY`). If that changes and the keys are
not re-entered, the stored secret can no longer be decrypted and the organisation silently reads
as "AI is nog niet ingesteld" (AI is not set up yet). Paste the keys again in *Instellingen → AI*.
</Aside>
